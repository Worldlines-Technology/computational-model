### PAPERS PROCESSED

**Paper 1: "Single-minus graviton tree amplitudes are nonzero"**
ArXiv ID: 2603.04330v1 | Selection type: strain

The framework claims reality is computation that updates via discrete ticks, with the speed of light as the maximum propagation rate. This paper reveals that graviton scattering amplitudes previously presumed to vanish are in fact nonzero in Klein space (signature (2,2)) or for complexified momenta, and that in a restricted kinematic region, these amplitudes factorize into products of soft factors governed by a recursive $\mathcal{L}w_{1+\infty}$ Ward identity. This strains the framework in three distinct ways. First, the nonvanishing of these amplitudes requires leaving Lorentzian signature — Klein space has *two* time dimensions, directly engaging T013 (time dimensionality as unexplained primitive). The framework's Tick primitive is defined around a single time direction; the physical content here lives in a signature where there are two tick directions, and the results are then analytically continued back to physical kinematics. The framework has no mechanism for accommodating computations that require passage through multi-time signature to yield physically meaningful results. Second, the $\mathcal{L}w_{1+\infty}$ symmetry algebra is an infinite-dimensional algebraic structure that *generates* the graviton amplitudes recursively from a three-point seed. This is precisely the kind of algebraic organizational structure flagged in T018 — the framework has no primitive capturing the algebraic hierarchy that organizes gravitational computation. Third, and most sharply, the fact that the physical content (amplitudes) is determined by a Ward identity of an infinite-dimensional symmetry algebra, not by dynamical evolution or gradient descent, reinforces T032 (symmetry as a structural primitive the framework lacks) and T026 (the structural content gap). The generating principle is algebraic symmetry, not any of the five dynamical primitives.

**Paper 2: "Non-Hermitian Quantum Mechanics with Applications to Gravity"**
ArXiv ID: 2603.04375v1 | Selection type: strain

The framework's Conservation of Computation primitive identifies unitarity (information conservation) as the most fundamental conservation law. This paper argues that Hermiticity — and therefore unitarity — is not a foundational axiom but a *symmetry law* arising from conservation of an inner product current, which can be obstructed by causal horizons. For observers restricted to one side of a black hole horizon, the dynamics become effectively non-Hermitian with completely positive trace-preserving (CPTP) evolution, and the generalized second law of thermodynamics reinterprets entropy production as compensating for inner product flux through the horizon. This directly and deeply strains Conservation of Computation. The framework treats unitarity as absolute — "computation cannot be created or destroyed." But if unitarity is a symmetry that can be broken by causal structure (horizons), then Conservation of Computation is not a universal law but a *conditional* law that holds only for observers with access to complete Cauchy surfaces. The Bianchi identity and Raychaudhuri equation provide the geometric mechanism by which this compensation operates, meaning the enforcement mechanism is geometric, not computational. The framework has no account of observer-dependent conservation — its conservation law is stated as ontological ("computation cannot be created or destroyed"), not perspectival ("for observers with full causal access, computation is conserved"). Furthermore, the paper's unification of gravity, entropy production, and effective non-Hermiticity under a single structural principle — inner product current conservation and its obstruction — represents exactly the kind of structural content (T026) the framework cannot express. The framework's Resolution primitive says resolution is irreversible and generates the arrow of time; this paper says the arrow of time (entropy production) is the *compensation* for information loss through horizons, giving it a geometric/causal origin rather than a computational one. This is a concrete challenge to the framework's claim that the arrow of time is "the accumulation of resolved states."

**Paper 3: "Wave-Optics Imprints of Dark Matter Subhalos on Strongly Lensed Gravitational Waves"**
ArXiv ID: 2603.04267v1 | Selection type: validation

The framework describes dark matter as "computational structure that couples to mass-density gradients (gravity) but not electromagnetic gradients." This paper computes the wave-optics signatures of CDM subhalo populations on gravitationally lensed gravitational waves, finding percent-level frequency-dependent amplitude and phase distortions in the LISA band from subhalos in the $10^4$–$10^7 M_\odot$ range — a mass range inaccessible to electromagnetic probes. The framework has genuine post-hoc consistency here: the reason GW lensing probes dark matter at scales electromagnetic observations cannot is precisely *because* dark matter couples gravitationally but not electromagnetically. The framework's description correctly predicts that gravitational channels (GW lensing) would reveal dark matter structure invisible to EM channels. However, this is post-hoc consistency, not genuine predictive validation. The framework did not predict the specific mass range, the percent-level distortion magnitude, the frequency dependence, or the wave-optics (rather than geometric-optics) character of the signatures. Standard ΛCDM + GR makes all the same predictions with far more quantitative precision. The framework's contribution is purely interpretive: "this is what we would expect if dark matter is computation coupled to gravity but not EM." But every CDM model says the same thing in more specific language. The model note (from T020) that the dark matter coupling should be understood as quantitative rather than categorical is not tested here, since this paper assumes standard CDM with strictly zero electromagnetic coupling. The validation is real but shallow — consistency with a qualitative prediction that any viable dark matter theory also makes.

### FRAMEWORK STRAINS

The deepest strain this cycle comes from Paper 2. The framework's Conservation of Computation is stated as an ontological absolute: "Computation cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed." Trivedi et al. demonstrate that unitarity — the framework's "most fundamental expression" of this conservation law — is properly understood as a *conditional symmetry* that holds when the inner product current is globally conserved, which requires complete Cauchy surfaces without boundary flux. In the presence of horizons, the conservation law is *obstructed*, and the dynamics for restricted observers are genuinely non-unitary (CPTP). The framework cannot accommodate this because it has no concept of observer-dependent conservation. It treats conservation as a view-from-nowhere law about the total computational content of reality. But if "total computational content" is only well-defined for observers with access to complete Cauchy surfaces — and physically, no observer has access to a complete Cauchy surface (every observer has a cosmological horizon) — then Conservation of Computation is never exactly true for any physical observer. The framework would need to say something like "Conservation of Computation holds for the universe as a whole but not for any subsystem bounded by a horizon," but this transforms it from an operational principle (testable from within) to a metaphysical commitment about the whole (untestable from within), dramatically weakening its explanatory power. Furthermore, the geometric mechanism (Bianchi identity + Raychaudhuri equation) that enforces the entropy-information balance is purely structural — it lives in the geometry of spacetime, not in any computational process — adding further weight to T026 and T032.

The strain from Paper 1 is more specific but also significant: the physical relevance of Klein space (2,2) signature means that the framework's Tick primitive, which axiomatically assumes one time dimension, is not just an unexplained choice (T013) but an active obstruction to describing known physics. The graviton amplitudes are nonzero only when we allow two time directions, and they contribute to physical Lorentzian results via analytic continuation. The framework has no mechanism for any of this. The computation "goes through" a signature the framework cannot describe.

### STATE UPDATES

1. **Conservation of Computation — downgrade from absolute to conditional.** The Conservation of Computation description should be updated to note: unitarity (information conservation) is properly understood as a symmetry arising from conservation of an inner product current on complete Cauchy surfaces. In the presence of causal horizons, this conservation is obstructed for restricted observers, yielding genuinely non-unitary (CPTP) effective dynamics. Conservation of Computation holds globally (for the full Cauchy surface) but is broken for any subsystem bounded by a horizon. The generalized second law provides the compensating mechanism: entropy production at the horizon exactly balances the inner product flux. This means Conservation of Computation, as experienced by any finite observer, is approximate — the exact law holds only for the universe as a whole, if such a global perspective is well-defined. The arrow of time (previously attributed purely to "accumulation of resolved states") has a complementary geometric/causal origin: it is the compensation for information loss through horizons.

2. **Tick primitive — flag Klein space relevance.** The model should note that physical graviton amplitudes require passage through Klein (2,2) signature for certain kinematic configurations (single-minus helicity, half-collinear kinematics). The Tick primitive's assumption of one time dimension is not just unexplained (T013) but actively prevents the framework from describing intermediate steps in physically relevant computations. The analytic continuation between signatures should be noted as a structural feature of the gravitational computation that has no expression in the framework.

3. **Dark matter claim — note GW lensing as a consistency check.** The model should record that wave-optics lensing of gravitational waves by CDM subhalos (Ando 2025) is consistent with the framework's description of dark matter as structure coupling gravitationally but not electromagnetically, but that this is post-hoc consistency shared with all CDM models and does not constitute differential validation.

4. **Model — note infinite-dimensional symmetry algebras as generating structures.** The $\mathcal{L}w_{1+\infty}$ Ward identity generating graviton amplitudes should be recorded as a concrete example where the organizational structure of physics is an infinite-dimensional algebra that recursively generates all amplitudes from a seed, with the algebra being neither a conserved quantity nor a gradient nor an attractor but a structural generator. This strengthens T018 and T026.

### NEW TENSIONS

**T035 — Conservation of Computation is observer-dependent**
**Opened:** this cycle
**Description:** Trivedi, Gurrola & Scherrer (2603.04375) demonstrate that Hermiticity (and therefore unitarity) is a symmetry law arising from global conservation of an inner product current, not a foundational axiom. In the presence of causal horizons, this conservation is obstructed, and restricted observers experience genuinely non-unitary (CPTP) dynamics. The framework's Conservation of Computation primitive states that "computation cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed" — an absolute, observer-independent law. If unitarity is the framework's "most fundamental expression" of this law, and unitarity is observer-dependent (holding globally but broken for horizon-bounded observers), then Conservation of Computation as stated is either (a) true only globally and inaccessible to any finite observer, or (b) needs reformulation as a balance law where computational loss through horizons is compensated by entropy production at the horizon surface, with the Bianchi identity and Raychaudhuri equation providing the geometric enforcement. Option (b) would mean conservation is enforced by geometric structure, not by computation itself — the geometry *guarantees* the balance, and the computation merely *participates* in it. This inverts the framework's ontological priority (computation first, geometry emergent) and aligns with T026/T032's observation that structural/geometric content is missing from the primitives. The paper's proposal that black hole ringdown observations could bound the strength of inner product flux means this tension is empirically accessible, not just philosophical.
**Why unresolved:** The framework cannot accommodate observer-dependent conservation without major restructuring. If Conservation of Computation is weakened to "holds globally," it becomes unfalsifiable (no observer can verify the global balance). If reformulated as a horizon balance law, the enforcement mechanism is geometric (Raychaudhuri + Bianchi), not computational, undermining the framework's computational ontology. If Conservation of Computation is conditional on causal structure, a theory of causal structure must precede the conservation law, but the framework currently derives causal structure (light cones, horizons) from the Tick primitive and derives conservation independently — they are not ordered. The framework needs a causal-conservation coupling it does not have.

**T036 — Multi-signature computation: physical results require passage through disallowed signatures**
**Opened:** this cycle

(2603.04330) show that single-minus graviton tree amplitudes, previously presumed to vanish in Lorentzian signature, are nonzero in Klein space (signature (2,2)) or for complexified momenta, and contribute to physical results via analytic continuation. The $\mathcal{L}w_{1+\infty}$ Ward identity that generates these amplitudes operates in the complexified/Klein kinematic space. The framework's Tick primitive assumes one time dimension, and all five primitives are formulated for Lorentzian signature. If physically relevant computation passes through Klein signature as an intermediate step — even if the inputs and outputs are Lorentzian — the framework's computational substrate must either (a) natively support multiple signatures (contradicting the single-Tick axiom), (b) support complexified kinematics (which have no clear interpretation in terms of discrete ticks updating real physical states), or (c) treat the Klein/complex intermediate as a mathematical convenience with no ontological status (but the framework claims reality *is* computation, so if the computation goes through Klein space, that's where reality is during that step). This tension is related to T013 (time dimensionality) but sharper: T013 notes that the number of time dimensions is unexplained; T036 notes that physical graviton amplitudes *require* multi-time intermediate computation, making the single-time axiom not just unexplained but functionally incomplete.
**Why unresolved:** The framework has no concept of "intermediate computational signature." Analytic continuation between spacetime signatures is a standard technique in mathematical physics but has no interpretation in the framework's computational ontology. If the substrate computes in discrete ticks (one time direction), what does it mean for an amplitude to be nonzero only when there are two time directions? Either the substrate has richer signature structure than the framework admits, or the amplitude calculation is a mathematical representation that does not map directly onto the substrate's computation — but the latter option undermines the "reality is literally computation" claim.

**T037 — Hermiticity as emergent symmetry inverts the framework's ontological ordering**
**Opened:** this cycle
**Description:** Paper 2 argues that Hermiticity is not a foundational axiom but an emergent symmetry law — the special case of globally conserved inner product current. The framework treats the real-valuedness of physical observables and the unitarity of evolution as consequences of Conservation of Computation (a primitive). If Hermiticity is instead emergent from geometric/causal structure (requiring complete Cauchy surfaces), then the framework's ordering is inverted: conservation is not primitive but derived from spacetime geometry. The paper shows that the Bianchi identity (a geometric identity) and the Raychaudhuri equation (a geometric focusing theorem) together enforce the entropy-information balance, meaning *geometry guarantees conservation*, not the other way around. This directly challenges the framework's claim that geometry (spacetime structure, curvature) emerges from the computational substrate. If conservation emerges from geometry rather than geometry emerging from computation, the framework's explanatory direction is backwards for this phenomenon. Combined with the existing challenge from T016 (geometric encoding vs. computational ontology) and T026 (structural content gap), this forms a pattern: geometric structure keeps appearing as *prior to* or *independent of* computational structure, contrary to the framework's ontological ordering.
**Why unresolved:** The framework would need to either (a) demonstrate that the Bianchi identity and Raychaudhuri equation themselves emerge from the computational substrate in a way that the inner product conservation then follows (preserving the framework's ordering but requiring substantial new derivations), (b) accept that geometry and computation are co-fundamental rather than computation being prior (weakening the core ontological claim), or (c) argue that the geometric enforcement of conservation is itself a form of computation (which risks trivializing "computation" into meaninglessness, as flagged by T021). None of these has been attempted.
