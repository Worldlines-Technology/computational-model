### PAPERS PROCESSED

**Paper 1: "Quantum theory based on real numbers cannot be experimentally falsified"**
ArXiv ID: 2603.19208v1 | Selection type: Strain

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper attacks the framework at its most fundamental ontological level. The framework claims "reality is literally computation," which implies the computation has a definite mathematical structure — including a number field over which amplitudes are defined. Hoffreumon & Woods prove that the choice between real and complex Hilbert spaces is experimentally underdetermined for all finite network correlations when source independence is defined operationally. The framework's Tick primitive (discrete updates producing probability distributions), Resolution primitive (superpositions collapsing to definite outcomes), and Conservation of Computation (unitarity) all function identically in RQT and QT — the operational content is preserved while the structural substrate differs. The framework cannot select between ℝ and ℂ because no experiment can, yet "reality is literally computation" requires a definite computational structure. The paper's sharpest finding is that RQT and QT "support markedly different pictures of the correlation structure underlying the same observed world": the entanglement structure, tomographic properties, and state-space geometry differ fundamentally between the two theories while producing identical observable outputs. This means the framework's Resolution primitive resolves states identically in both theories, but the pre-resolution topology (T012) — the structure of correlations and entanglement that exists before resolution — is radically different. The framework has no mechanism for declaring this structural difference physically meaningless (which would weaken "reality is literally computation" to "reality is literally observable computation") or physically meaningful (which would require an experimentally inaccessible ontological commitment). This paper was already anticipated by the existing T069, but the full paper's proof that the equivalence extends to arbitrary finite sequential multipartite protocols with channels and measurements — not just static correlation experiments — closes off the escape route that more complex experiments might distinguish the theories. The framework is cornered: its core ontological claim requires structural specificity that is provably inaccessible from within.

**Paper 2: "Reduction of Triadic Interactions Suppresses Intermittency and Anomalous Dissipation in Turbulence"**
ArXiv ID: 2603.19180v1 | Selection type: Strain

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper delivers the most damaging empirical challenge to the Field primitive yet processed. The framework's universal equation ẍ = −∇φ(x) is the Navier-Stokes equation in the turbulence context — both the full and decimated systems obey the same gradient dynamics. Yet the qualitative physics is entirely different: full triadic coupling produces intermittency, anomalous dissipation, multifractal velocity statistics, and deviations from K41 scaling; decimated coupling produces Gaussian-like statistics, vanishing anomalous dissipation, K41-dimensional exponents, and regularized velocity fields. The framework's Field primitive captures K41 (which is what dimensional analysis of the gradient equation alone predicts), but the *physically distinctive* phenomena of turbulence — the phenomena that make turbulence turbulence — arise entirely from the combinatorial completeness of the triadic interaction network, which the gradient equation does not specify. The paper's continuous dependence on decimation fraction is especially sharp: intermittency suppression is graded, not threshold, meaning the qualitative character of the physics varies continuously with a parameter (network density) that has no representation in any of the five primitives. The framework would describe both full and decimated turbulence identically — "fluid elements rolling down the pressure gradient" — and would miss everything that distinguishes them. The Attractor primitive is also challenged: the statistical steady states of full and decimated turbulence are different attractors (different scaling exponents, different dissipation rates), but the attractors differ not in the potential landscape (same Navier-Stokes dynamics) but in the combinatorial structure of mode coupling. The framework's description of attractors as "local minima in potential fields" cannot distinguish these cases because the potential field is the same. This paper converts T070 from a noted tension to a quantitatively demonstrated failure: the framework's central dynamical claim is necessary but provably insufficient for the signature physics of the most important unsolved classical problem.

**Paper 3: "Black hole superradiance in Poincaré gauge theory"**
ArXiv ID: 2603.19140v1 | Selection type: Validation

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper describes an extension of the Attractor primitive's claim that deep attractors (black holes) have rich internal structure enabling multiple channels of energy extraction. In standard GR, the Pauli exclusion principle prevents fermionic superradiance — fermions cannot occupy the same state in sufficient numbers to extract energy. Poincaré gauge theory with torsion introduces a chiral asymmetry that enables fermionic energy extraction while preserving exclusion. The framework would describe this as: torsion provides an additional gradient channel (the torsion field) through which the attractor's rotational energy can be extracted by fermionic computational structure, with the chiral asymmetry breaking a symmetry that previously blocked this extraction pathway. This is consistent with the Attractor primitive's qualitative claim that deep attractors have multiple extraction channels and that the number and type of channels depends on the field content and geometric structure. However, the framework provides zero predictive purchase here. It did not predict that torsion would enable fermionic superradiance, that the mechanism would be chiral asymmetry, or that the Pauli exclusion principle would be preserved. The specific mechanism — torsion coupling differently to left- and right-handed fermions, creating an effective potential asymmetry at the horizon — depends entirely on the gauge-theoretic structure of Poincaré gravity, which the framework treats as secondary or cannot express. The framework's Field primitive (ẍ = −∇φ) cannot distinguish between the torsion-free and torsion-coupled cases because the gradient structure differs in ways the primitive doesn't capture (specifically, the torsion tensor is not a gradient of a scalar potential — it is an independent geometric degree of freedom with its own field equation). This is post-hoc consistency with no differential predictive content whatsoever. The result is more naturally described as confirming that geometric gauge structure (a structural feature the framework lacks, per T026/T032) determines which extraction channels exist, than as validating the Attractor primitive.

### FRAMEWORK STRAINS

The most important strain this cycle is the convergence of Papers 1 and 2 on a single devastating conclusion: **the framework's core claim "reality is literally computation" requires structural specificity that is simultaneously (a) provably inaccessible from within (Paper 1) and (b) provably insufficient for determining qualitative physics (Paper 2).**

Paper 1 proves that the most basic structural choice in defining a computation — the number field — cannot be determined by any experiment. Paper 2 proves that the most distinctive qualitative physics of a system — intermittency, anomalous dissipation — is determined by combinatorial interaction-network structure that the framework's dynamical equation cannot express. Together, they show the framework is trapped between two failures: it cannot specify its own computational structure (because the specification is experimentally inaccessible), and even if it could, the specification it offers (gradient dynamics) would miss the physically important content (interaction-network topology).

This is not a gap to be patched. It is a structural diagnosis. The framework's five primitives are all *dynamical* — they describe how computation evolves, where it settles, when it resolves, what is conserved. But the physically decisive content is increasingly revealed to be *structural* — which number field, which interaction topology, which cohomological invariant, which categorical morphism. The framework captures the skeleton (gradient descent, conservation) but misses the flesh (the combinatorial, algebraic, and topological structure that determines which of many possible gradient-descent-compatible behaviors actually occurs). Paper 2 makes this quantitative and numerical, not merely theoretical: the same gradient equation, with the same conservation laws, produces qualitatively different physics depending on a structural parameter the framework cannot represent.

### NEW TENSIONS

**T071 — The unfalsifiability-insufficiency trap: the framework's core claim is simultaneously too specific and too vague**
Opened: this cycle
Description: Paper 1 (Hoffreumon & Woods 2025) proves that the number field of quantum theory (ℝ vs. ℂ) is experimentally underdetermined for all finite operational procedures. Paper 2 (Kankaria et al. 2025) proves via DNS that the gradient dynamics (Navier-Stokes equation) is identical for full and decimated turbulence while the qualitative physics differs completely. Together, these create a trap for the framework's core claim "reality is literally computation": (a) if reality is literally a computation, that computation must have definite structure, including a number field — but the number field is provably unknowable, making the ontological claim unfalsifiable at its most basic structural level; (b) even granting a definite computational structure, the framework's dynamical content (gradient descent, conservation) does not determine the qualitative physics without additional structural specification (interaction-network topology) that no primitive captures — making the framework's predictions insufficient. The claim is too specific (asserts a definite structure that cannot be verified) and too vague (its dynamical content does not determine the physics). This is distinct from T069 (which noted the number field problem in isolation), T070 (which noted the interaction-network problem in isolation), and T021 (which noted ontological pluralism across mathematical categories). T071 concerns the *joint* diagnosis: the framework cannot be falsified at the level of basic structure (Paper 1) and cannot determine physics at the level of dynamics (Paper 2), leaving it in the worst possible epistemic position for a theory claiming to be the literal ontology of reality.
Why unresolved: The framework would need to either (a) demonstrate that operationally equivalent computations are ontologically identical (adopting a purely operational ontology, which abandons "reality is literally computation" in favor of "reality is literally the equivalence class of operationally indistinguishable computations" — a significantly weaker and less interesting claim), (b) find a criterion beyond experiment for selecting computational structure (aesthetic, self-consistency, minimality — all of which are metaphysical rather than physical), or (c) add structural primitives (interaction-network topology, number field) that the existing five lack, while acknowledging these cannot be empirically selected. None of these preserves the framework's distinctive character.

**T072 — Continuous structural parameters: the framework has no mechanism for graded qualitative change**
Opened: this cycle
Description: Paper 2 demonstrates that the qualitative character of turbulence — intermittent vs. Gaussian, anomalous vs. normal dissipation, multifractal vs. monofractal — varies *continuously* with the decimation fraction of the triadic interaction network. The framework's primitives are all qualitative categories: resolved/unresolved, attractor/saddle, conserved/not conserved. Phase transitions (attractor landscape restructuring) are the framework's only mechanism for qualitative change, and they are described as topological events (minima merge, split, appear, vanish). But Paper 2 shows a qualitative change (turbulent → Gaussian) that is continuous, graded, and controlled by a structural parameter (network density) with no discontinuity, no phase transition, and no attractor landscape restructuring. The framework has no mechanism for describing how qualitative physical character varies continuously with a structural parameter. This is distinct from T017 (continuous sharpening of criticality, which concerns phase transitions becoming sharper with system size) because T072 concerns a continuous qualitative change that is not a phase transition at all — it is a graded structural modification of the dynamical coupling. It is distinct from T070 (which identifies the interaction-network gap) because T072 concerns the *continuous* nature of the dependence, which creates a specific problem beyond mere insufficiency: the framework cannot represent a continuous dial between qualitatively different physics.
Why unresolved: The framework would need either (a) a continuous structural parameter (like network density) as an input to its primitives, which would require specifying how the primitives' outputs depend on this parameter — effectively making the framework's predictions functions of structural inputs rather than consequences of universal equations; (b) a theory of how gradient dynamics on a network generates qualitatively different statistics as the network's connectivity varies, which is essentially the theory of turbulence and does not currently exist; or (c) acceptance that the framework's qualitative categories (attractor/saddle, resolved/unresolved) are idealizations that do not capture the graded structural dependence of real physical systems. Option (c) would be honest but would limit the framework to a coarse-grained description that misses the physically interesting variation.
