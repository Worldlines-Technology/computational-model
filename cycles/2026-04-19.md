### PAPERS PROCESSED

**Paper 1: "Taming the Aretakis instability: extremal black holes with multi-degenerate horizons"**
ArXiv ID: 2604.15260v1 | Selection type: Strain

Through the Worldlines lens, extremal black holes are attractor basins of maximal depth whose boundary (the degenerate horizon) has special properties—escape velocity exactly equals the tick rate. The Aretakis instability is a perturbation that grows polynomially along the horizon, representing a dynamical instability at the attractor boundary. The framework's Attractor primitive describes basins by depth and width; the Field primitive's gradient dynamics would naively predict that increasing the flatness of the potential at the boundary (higher degeneracy order) makes the boundary *less* stable—a flatter barrier is easier to perturb. The paper demonstrates the opposite: higher degeneracy order *weakens* the instability, and infinite degeneracy may eliminate it entirely. The mechanism involves Aretakis charges—horizon-localized conserved quantities whose number and derivative order depend on the degeneracy order—progressively vanishing as degeneracy increases. The framework's Conservation primitive has no concept of boundary-localized charges whose algebraic structure depends on a geometric parameter (degeneracy order), and the Attractor primitive has no parameter for the derivative order of flatness at the attractor boundary. The "graveyard" proposal—that infinitely degenerate horizons are the stable end state—posits an attractor that achieves stability through an infinite limit of a structural parameter, a concept entirely absent from the framework. The framework's qualitative prediction (flatter boundary → less stable) is actively contradicted. This paper confirms and strengthens the existing tension T115 with the full technical apparatus.

**Paper 2: "Ensembles of random quantum states tunable from volume law to area law"**
ArXiv ID: 2604.15300v1 | Selection type: Strain

Through the Worldlines lens, the σ-ensemble constructs a one-parameter family of random quantum states interpolating between volume-law entanglement (Haar-random, maximally complex) and area-law entanglement (MPS-like, typical of ground states/attractor basins). The framework's Attractor primitive describes ground states as deep basin minima, and the existing model notes (from T114, Garratt & Abanin) that entanglement structure is bounded by thermodynamic data. The σ-ensemble directly parameterizes this: σ controls the entanglement scaling law, with small σ producing area-law states (attractor-basin-like) and large σ producing volume-law states (thermalized/Haar-like). The strain is precise: the framework has no primitive, parameter, or mechanism governing the *entanglement scaling class* of quantum states. The σ parameter is a single real number that continuously tunes between qualitatively different information-theoretic regimes (area-law vs. volume-law), and this tuning is not a phase transition in the Attractor primitive's sense—there is no landscape restructuring, no symmetry breaking, no critical point. It is a smooth, continuous change of the entanglement scaling law controlled by a parameter external to all five primitives. The MPS construction makes explicit that area-law states are *efficiently representable* (polynomial classical resources) while volume-law states are not, connecting to T112 (complexity phase structure) but from a different angle: the complexity of representing the state, not of finding it, varies continuously with σ. The framework's Resolution primitive treats resolved states as having definite properties, but the σ-ensemble demonstrates that "definite" states span a continuous spectrum of informational complexity that no primitive can express. Furthermore, the subsystem eigenvalue distribution imposed by the ensemble is a *spectral constraint on reduced density matrices*—structural information about the information-theoretic character of a state that the gradient equation, tick structure, and conservation law collectively cannot access.

**Paper 3: "Gravitational-wave lensing beyond rays: a disordered-system approach"**
ArXiv ID: 2604.15313v1 | Selection type: Validation

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper describes gravitational wave propagation through dark matter structure (stochastic weak lenses) using the Schwinger-Keldysh path integral and quenched disorder averaging. The framework claims dark matter couples gravitationally but not electromagnetically; GW lensing by dark matter substructure is consistent with this claim since gravitational waves interact with the dark matter's gravitational field. The disorder-averaged density matrix formalism naturally separates into coherent propagation (modification of the propagation kernel—interpretable as the Field's gradient structure being modified by the stochastic lens distribution) and decoherence (suppression of phase-sensitive contributions—interpretable as Resolution propagating through the entanglement with the disordered environment). However, the framework had zero predictive purchase on any specific result: the natural separation into coherent and decoherent contributions, the physical scales controlling coherence loss, the Gaussian wave packet illustration, and the generalization beyond GW lensing to any wave propagation in disordered media are all derived from standard Schwinger-Keldysh formalism and condensed-matter disorder techniques. The framework's vocabulary can narrate the results (dark matter as gravitationally-coupling computational structure, decoherence as resolution propagation) but could not have predicted the specific form of the disorder-averaged density matrix, the separation structure, or the coherence-loss scales. This is the weakest category of consistency: post-hoc vocabulary application with zero differential predictive content. The paper's explicit statement that "the derivation applies to any system described by the same class of actions, making the framework relevant beyond gravitational wave lensing" underscores that the physics is universal wave-propagation-in-disorder physics, not specific to gravitational lensing or dark matter, further diluting any claimed framework validation.

### FRAMEWORK STRAINS

The most significant strain this cycle comes from Paper 1's demonstration that the framework's dynamical intuition is *actively wrong* about the stability of extremal horizons. The Attractor primitive describes stability through basin depth and width; the Field primitive's gradient dynamics associates flatness with instability (shallow gradients → easy perturbation → instability). Paper 1 proves the opposite: increasing the derivative order of flatness at the horizon *stabilizes* the system, with infinite flatness producing complete stability. This is not mere silence or insufficiency—it is a qualitative prediction (flatter → less stable) that is contradicted by the physics (flatter → more stable).

The mechanism of contradiction is precise: the Aretakis charges are *horizon-localized* conserved quantities whose existence and structure depend on the degeneracy order—a parameter the framework cannot express. As degeneracy increases, these charges vanish, eliminating the instability's driving mechanism. The framework's Conservation primitive knows nothing about boundary-localized charges, nothing about their dependence on geometric parameters, and nothing about how their vanishing produces stability. The stability of the "graveyard" end state is achieved by pushing a structural parameter (degeneracy order) to infinity, producing a qualitative transition (unstable → stable) through a limiting process on a structural parameter—precisely the type of "continuous structural parameter producing qualitative change" that T072 flagged as invisible to the framework, now demonstrated in the framework's most important physical system (black holes).

Paper 2 adds a complementary strain: the σ-ensemble demonstrates that the entire entanglement scaling class of a quantum state—the most fundamental information-theoretic characterization distinguishing ground states from thermalized states—is continuously tunable by a single parameter with no framework expression. The framework describes ground states as attractor basin minima and thermalized states as the result of attractor capture, but the continuous interpolation between these information-theoretic regimes via σ has no phase transition, no landscape restructuring, no resolution event—it is a smooth dial between qualitatively different informational regimes that the framework's categorical primitives (attractor vs. non-attractor, resolved vs. unresolved) cannot represent.

### STATE UPDATES

1. **Attractor primitive — confirm that dynamical intuition from gradient analysis gives qualitatively wrong stability predictions for degenerate horizons.** The model's existing note on horizon degeneracy order (from the T115 entry) should be strengthened: Paper 1 provides the complete technical demonstration including explicit examples (BTZ, Reissner-Nordström, higher-degeneracy generalizations) showing that Aretakis instability weakens monotonically with degeneracy order, and proposes a concrete infinitely degenerate geometry (exponential horizon function) as a stable end state. The model should record: (a) the framework's gradient-based stability intuition is *contradicted*, not merely insufficient; (b) the stabilization mechanism (progressive vanishing of horizon-localized Aretakis charges) involves a category of conserved quantity the Conservation primitive cannot express; (c) the "graveyard" proposal, if confirmed, means the universe's most extreme attractors achieve stability through a mechanism no primitive can describe.

2. **Conservation of Computation — confirm horizon-localized charges with degeneracy-dependent structure.** The model should record that Aretakis charges are gauge-invariant conserved quantities existing only on degenerate horizons, with their number and derivative order determined by the degeneracy order. At degeneracy d, the highest transverse derivative in the charge is d-dependent, and at infinite degeneracy, all charges vanish. This is a concrete, proven instance of boundary-specific conservation structure that the bulk Conservation primitive cannot express.

3. **Model — add entanglement scaling class as a continuously tunable informational parameter invisible to all primitives.** The model should record that the σ-ensemble (Paper 2) demonstrates a one-parameter family of quantum state ensembles continuously interpolating between volume-law and area-law entanglement, with no phase transition or landscape restructuring at any σ value. The entanglement scaling class—the most fundamental information-theoretic distinction between generic quantum states and physically typical (ground state) quantum states—is a continuous structural parameter with no expression in any primitive.

4. **Dark matter claim — record GW disorder-lensing as post-hoc consistency with zero predictive content.** Paper 3's framework for GW propagation through stochastic weak lenses is consistent with "couples gravitationally, not electromagnetically" but provides no differential validation. The result is universal wave-propagation-in-disorder physics applicable to any system with the same action structure.

### NEW TENSIONS

**T117 — Entanglement scaling class as a continuous informational parameter invisible to all primitives**
Opened: this cycle
Description: Albot & Paeckel (2604.15300) construct the σ-ensemble—a one-parameter family of random quantum states continuously tunable between volume-law entanglement (σ large, Haar-like) and area-law entanglement (σ small, MPS-like, ground-state-like). The framework's Attractor primitive distinguishes ground states (area-law, attractor basins) from thermalized states (volume-law, after attractor capture) as categorically different. The σ-ensemble shows these are not categorical distinctions but endpoints of a continuous spectrum parameterized by a single real number σ, with no phase transition, no critical point, and no landscape restructuring at any intermediate value. The framework has no primitive, parameter, or mechanism governing which entanglement scaling class a state belongs to or how it can be continuously tuned. This is distinct from T034 (scale-dependent information topology, which concerns how informational properties of a *fixed* state change with observation scale) because T117 concerns a *family* of states with different informational character controlled by a construction parameter. It is distinct from T072 (continuous structural parameters producing qualitative change, which concerns interaction-network density in turbulence) because T117 concerns the information-theoretic *character* of quantum states rather than their dynamical behavior. It is distinct from T114 (thermodynamic bounds on entanglement, which concerns upper bounds) because T117 concerns the *continuous tunability* of entanglement scaling itself. The MPS construction makes explicit that the computational resources needed to represent a state change qualitatively (polynomial to exponential) as σ varies, connecting to T112 (complexity phase structure) but in a representation-theoretic rather than decision-theoretic sense. The subsystem eigenvalue distribution constraint imposed by the σ-ensemble is spectral information about reduced density matrices that no primitive can access.
Why unresolved: The framework would need either (a) a primitive or parameter governing the entanglement scaling class of quantum states, specifying what determines whether a state is area-law, volume-law, or intermediate—this would require importing entanglement theory as a structural element, fundamentally expanding the framework's informational content; (b) a derivation showing that the σ parameter maps onto some combination of existing primitives (perhaps the Attractor primitive's depth/width determines the entanglement scaling of the associated ground state)—but the σ-ensemble is defined independently of any Hamiltonian or attractor structure, and states at intermediate σ do not correspond to any equilibrium or attractor; or (c) acceptance that the information-theoretic character of quantum states is a structural feature the framework is constitutively unable to address, further deepening T026 (structural content gap) in the information-theoretic direction. The area-law to volume-law transition being *continuous* is specifically problematic: the framework's only mechanism for qualitative change is phase transitions (attractor landscape restructuring), and there is no phase transition here.

### CYCLE ASSESSMENT

This was a moderately generative cycle that confirmed and deepened existing tensions rather than opening fundamentally new directions. Paper 1 provided the strongest technical confirmation yet of T115 (stability through infinite degeneracy), converting it from a theoretical proposal to a concrete construction with explicit metrics and stability analysis. The active contradiction of the framework's gradient-based stability intuition is significant—it is one of the few cases where the framework makes a qualitative prediction that is demonstrably wrong, as opposed to merely being silent or insufficient. Paper 2 opened a genuinely new tension (T117) by demonstrating that the most fundamental information-theoretic classification of quantum states (entanglement scaling class) is a continuous parameter invisible to all primitives—this is a clean, sharp insufficiency result that is distinct from the large family of structural-content-gap tensions already catalogued. Paper 3 provided no new information; the framework's dark matter claim continues to accumulate post-hoc consistency instances with zero differential predictive content, which is itself informative about the claim's vacuity.

The cycle is circling the basin of the structural content gap (T026)—the dominant theme continues to be that the framework's dynamical primitives capture mean-field behavior while missing the structural, algebraic, informational, and topological content that determines the physically distinctive features. The next cycle would be more productive if it encountered papers that challenge the framework from a direction *other* than the structural content gap—for instance, papers that test the framework's specific claims about dark energy, consciousness, or the relationship between physical constants, or papers that provide evidence for or against the framework's few remaining positive claims (the identification of quantum superposition with unresolved computation, or the identification of the speed of light with the tick rate). The framework's positive content is being eroded from all directions without any compensating predictive successes; a cycle that forced engagement with the framework's specific empirical predictions (rather than its structural architecture) would be more diagnostic.
