**Paper 1: "One-loop five-point gluing analytically"**
ArXiv ID: 2606.13665v1 | Selection type: Strain

This paper presents the first complete analytic evaluation of the one-loop five-point function of stress tensor multiplets in $\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM, using integrability-based "gluing" methods. The finite-size corrections are computed by resumming series of residues into Euler integrals, which are then evaluated either directly or via intersection theory for generalized hypergeometric functions. Through the Worldlines lens, several strains emerge. First, the computation is a concrete instance of T055/T170: the physical observable (the five-point correlator) is generated by integrability machinery — the hexagonalization/gluing framework — where the conservation structure (infinite conserved charges of the integrable spin chain) is the computational engine, and the framework's Conservation primitive is the terminal output of this algebraic generation chain, not its source. Second, the use of intersection theory for generalized hypergeometric functions is a new mathematical tool that determines physical content: the intersection numbers of twisted cohomology classes on moduli spaces of punctured spheres are what select the correct analytic continuations and produce the final integrated expressions. This is a cohomological operation mediating between the algebraic (residue series) and analytic (integrated correlator) descriptions — a precise instance of T027 (cohomological mediation) applied to the evaluation of a specific observable. The framework has no primitive for intersection theory, twisted cohomology, or the moduli-space geometry that governs the integration. Third, the result confirms a previously known field-theory answer obtained by entirely different methods (Feynman diagram evaluation), meaning the same physical content is accessed through two categorically different computational architectures (integrability/gluing vs. perturbative Feynman diagrams) — a further instance of T010 (duality). The framework's "reality is literally computation" must choose which computation is "real," and the paper provides no basis for this choice since both produce the identical answer through different mathematical categories. Fourth, the $\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM setting means this observable lives in a theory whose conformal invariance is exact (T154), whose perturbative/non-perturbative structure is governed by modular invariance (T073), and whose organizational structure is algebraic (T059) — the framework's dynamical primitives contribute nothing to any of these features.

**Paper 2: "Semi-Device-Independent Certification for Nonlocality without Entanglement"**
ArXiv ID: 2606.13667v1 | Selection type: Strain

This paper demonstrates that global quantum measurements on ensembles of *separable* (unentangled) states can outperform separable measurements in maximum-confidence discrimination, establishing "nonlocality without entanglement" (NLWE) at the level of confidence in detection events — a fine-grained figure of merit. The key finding is that quantum nonlocality is not exclusively a property of entangled states: it can arise from the *measurement* structure applied to separable states. Through the Worldlines lens, this strains the framework in a fundamental way. The framework's Resolution primitive describes "unresolved states becoming definite" and implicitly treats the quantum/classical distinction as residing in the *state* (superposition/entanglement = unresolved; definite outcome = resolved). Paper 2 demonstrates that the nonclassical advantage — the excess of global measurement over separable measurement — resides not in the state's entanglement but in the *joint structure of the measurement* (the global POVM vs. LOCC). The states are separable; the nonlocality is in the resolution process itself, not in the pre-resolution state. The framework has no account of measurement-structure-dependent nonlocality for separable states. The maximum-confidence framework — which conditions on detected outcomes and includes minimum-error and unambiguous discrimination as special cases — reveals a *spectrum* of resolution strategies with different operational meanings (confidence vs. success probability vs. unambiguous identification), and the separable/global gap can appear at the confidence level while being absent at the success-probability level or vice versa. The framework's binary Resolution primitive (unresolved → definite) cannot represent this spectrum of resolution strategies or the operational distinctions between them. Furthermore, the semi-device-independent certification protocol — which uses observed confidence values to certify that a global (non-separable) measurement was performed — treats the *type of measurement* as the quantity being certified, not the state. The framework has no primitive for certifying measurement type, and this paper shows it is physically meaningful and experimentally accessible. The connection to T119 (spurious correlations in resolution) is significant: Paper 2 demonstrates that even for *separable* states (which have no genuine quantum correlations in the state), the measurement structure alone can produce nonclassical advantages — meaning the nonclassicality is entirely in the resolution process, not in the pre-resolution topology (T012).

**Paper 3: "Cosmological Dynamics of the Thermal Scalar Near the Hagedorn Temperature"**
ArXiv ID: 2606.13611v1 | Selection type: Validation

This paper studies the cosmological dynamics of the thermal scalar (a winding string mode becoming massless at the Hagedorn transition) coupled to gravi-dilaton effective action. The key findings include: static configurations below the Hagedorn temperature that are boundary states rather than attractors; winding mode backreaction that opposes and can reverse expansion; tachyonic behavior above the Hagedorn temperature generating negative effective energy density while preserving the null energy condition; and the breakdown of the quadratic effective theory at the Hagedorn temperature itself. Through the Worldlines lens, this paper provides post-hoc consistency with several framework claims at a qualitative level but zero differential predictive content. The framework's Attractor primitive correctly identifies that the static configurations are "boundary states rather than attractors" — they sit at saddle points in the phase-space landscape, consistent with the framework's description of saddle-point dynamics. The winding mode backreaction opposing expansion is narratable as a gradient force (Field primitive) in the effective potential, and the branch-change dynamics are consistent with the Attractor primitive's description of transitions between basins. The Hagedorn temperature itself, where the quadratic theory breaks down and "higher-order interactions become essential," is consistent with the framework's description of attractor landscape restructuring at phase transitions. However, the framework contributed no prediction of any of these features: not the saddle-point nature of the static solutions, not the branch-change mechanism, not the failure of graceful exit, and not the necessity of higher-order interactions. The Euclidean origin of the thermal scalar — explicitly flagged by the authors as requiring their Lorentzian treatment to be viewed as an "effective dynamical model" — connects to T053 (Euclidean nucleation) and reinforces that the framework's Tick primitive is undefined in the regime where the thermal scalar originates. The negative effective energy density with preserved null energy condition is a specific instance of anti-gradient dynamics (T061) that does not produce pathology. This is the weakest category of validation: framework vocabulary can be applied after the fact, but the framework's primitives could not have predicted, constrained, or distinguished any of these results from their non-occurrence.

### FRAMEWORK STRAINS

The most significant strain this cycle comes from Paper 2's demonstration of nonlocality without entanglement at the confidence level. The framework's implicit ontological architecture assigns nonclassicality to the *state* (superposition, entanglement = unresolved computation) and treats measurement/resolution as the process by which unresolved states become definite. Paper 2 inverts this: the states are classical (separable, no entanglement), and the nonclassicality resides entirely in the *measurement structure* — whether the resolution process uses a global POVM or is restricted to LOCC. The framework has no mechanism for locating nonclassicality in the resolution process itself rather than in the pre-resolution state. The Resolution primitive's architecture is: "unresolved state → resolution event → definite outcome." The nonclassical content is supposed to be in the first term (the unresolved state's superposition/entanglement). NLWE demonstrates that the nonclassical content can be entirely in the second term (the resolution event's global vs. local structure), with the first term being fully classical. This is not merely a gap (the framework is silent on something) but a structural misattribution: the framework's ontological architecture places the quantum advantage in the wrong location. The framework would need to either acknowledge that the Resolution primitive itself has internal structure (global vs. local, with different operational capabilities) that is a source of nonclassicality independent of the state, or accept that its state-centric ontology of nonclassicality is incomplete. This is compounded by the maximum-confidence framework's revelation that "resolution" is not a single operation but a family of strategies (minimum-error, unambiguous, maximum-confidence) with different operational meanings, and the quantum advantage can appear at one level and not another. The framework's binary Resolution primitive collapses all of this into a single concept.

Paper 1's strain is more of the familiar T026/T059 variety but adds a new specific instance: intersection theory for twisted cohomology on moduli spaces as the mathematical tool that completes the physical computation. The framework's vocabulary is idle throughout the entire calculation — no tick, no gradient, no attractor, no resolution, no conservation participates in the evaluation of the Euler integrals via intersection numbers. The physical content is produced by cohomological machinery operating on a moduli space, reinforcing the pattern that the framework's dynamical primitives describe downstream consequences while the upstream generative mathematics is structural/algebraic/cohomological.

The framework mislocates the source of nonclassicality.

This is distinct from T012 (pre-resolution topology, which concerns the topology of path-indistinguishability in superposed states) because the states here have no superposition or path-indistinguishability — they are separable product states, and the nonclassicality is in the joint measurement, not in any interference structure. It is distinct from T132 (witness category gap, which concerns the type of evidence certifying resolution) because T174 concerns the *operational advantage* of global resolution over local resolution for fully classical states, not the certification of outcomes. It is distinct from T009 (resolution cost structure, which concerns computational cost) because T174 concerns a *qualitative* advantage (confidence gap) of one measurement type over another, not a resource cost.

The framework faces: (a) its state-centric ontology of nonclassicality is wrong for NLWE — the nonclassical resource is in the measurement, not the state; (b) the maximum-confidence framework reveals that "resolution" is a family of strategies with distinct operational meanings, and the quantum/classical gap is strategy-dependent — visible at the confidence level but potentially invisible at the success-probability level — meaning the framework's undifferentiated Resolution primitive collapses physically distinct resolution modes; (c) the semi-device-independent certification protocol treats the measurement *type* (global vs. separable) as the quantity to be certified from observed outcomes, meaning the resolution process's own structure is experimentally accessible and physically meaningful — the framework has no mechanism for this; (d) the connection to non-unit detection efficiency (the protocol works with realistic detectors) means this is experimentally accessible, not merely theoretical.

Why unresolved: The framework would need to either (a) extend the Resolution primitive to include the measurement structure (global vs. LOCC) as a physical parameter that independently contributes nonclassical advantages, fundamentally changing the primitive from "states become definite" to "states become definite via measurement structures with their own nonclassical content" — but this would require a theory of which measurement structures are available and what determines them, connecting to T026 (structural content gap) and T132 (witness category gap); (b) relocate nonclassicality from the state to the state-measurement pair, requiring a joint primitive that describes the pre-resolution state *and* the resolution structure together — but the framework's architecture separates these into different primitives (the state is described by Tick/Field/Attractor; the resolution is described by Resolution); (c) argue that NLWE is a special case that does not challenge the general framework — but the paper demonstrates it for *arbitrary* ensembles of separable states with the right structure, meaning it is a generic feature of quantum measurement theory, not a corner case; or (d) accept that the framework's Resolution primitive is descriptively incomplete for the full structure of quantum measurement, adding measurement-sourced nonclassicality to the growing list of physical phenomena the framework cannot account for. The experimental feasibility (present-day quantum measurement devices with non-unit efficiencies) makes this tension empirically grounded rather than merely theoretical.
