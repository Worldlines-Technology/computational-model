### PAPERS PROCESSED

**Paper 1: "A No-Cloning Trade-off Between Black Hole No-Hair and Horizon Smoothness"**
ArXiv ID: 2604.28050v1. Selection type: strain.

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper concerns the deepest attractor (black hole) and the interplay between Conservation of Computation (unitarity), Resolution (what becomes definite to exterior observers), and the Attractor primitive (horizon as the attractor boundary). The framework would narrate: the black hole is an attractor basin so deep that escape velocity exceeds the tick rate; infalling information crosses the horizon and enters the basin interior. The paper proves a quantitative trade-off: if the exterior computation can distinguish between two infalling states (exterior hair, $D_{\max} > 0$), then the interior channel must deviate from a perfect isometry by at least $\varepsilon \geq D_{\max}^2/8$, meaning the horizon is not perfectly smooth. The framework's strain is severe and specific. The trade-off is derived from three ingredients — unitarity, horizon causality, and interior accessibility — and the result is that Conservation of Computation (unitarity) is *in tension with* the Attractor primitive's description of the horizon as a smooth boundary. The framework treats black holes as attractors with well-defined boundaries; this paper proves the boundary cannot be simultaneously smooth (equivalence principle respected) and information-preserving (unitarity maintained) unless the infalling state is already entangled with the exterior. The framework has no mechanism for quantifying the degree to which an attractor boundary departs from smoothness, no primitive for the trade-off between conservation and attractor-boundary regularity, and no account of pre-existing entanglement as the resolution mechanism. The $2\sqrt{2\varepsilon}$ bound is a specific, quantitative relationship between conservation (unitarity), attractor boundary quality (horizon smoothness/interior fidelity), and resolution distinguishability (trace distance of exterior states) that the framework's qualitative primitives cannot express, derive, or approximate. Most critically, the paper demonstrates that the framework's two most fundamental primitives — Conservation (unitarity) and Attractor (horizon smoothness) — are in *quantitative tension with each other* at the deepest attractor, and the resolution of this tension involves information-theoretic structure (pre-existing entanglement, diamond norm channels, isometry fidelity) entirely outside the framework's vocabulary.

**Paper 2: "Order by disorder up to arbitrarily high temperature"**
ArXiv ID: 2604.28026v1. Selection type: strain.

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper concerns the Attractor primitive and the Field primitive's gradient dynamics. The framework says: things move down gradients; systems settle into energy minima; attractors are local minima in potential fields. Paper 2 rigorously proves the opposite for a class of lattice models: long-range checkerboard order persists to *arbitrarily high temperature* — including the $\beta \to 0$ (infinite temperature) limit — despite the checkerboard configurations *not being energy minimizers*. The ordering is purely entropic: the system is ordered not because it has fallen to the bottom of an energy gradient but because the ordered configurations have *more available microstates* (higher entropy, after partial trace over occupation numbers) than disordered ones. The framework's strain is categorical. The Field primitive $\dot{x} = -\nabla\phi(x)$ describes motion down energy gradients. The Attractor primitive defines attractors as "local minima in potential fields." This paper demonstrates stable, long-range ordered states that are (a) not energy minima, (b) selected by entropy not energy, (c) stable at *all* temperatures including infinite temperature, and (d) proven rigorously using Pirogov-Sinai theory with a Peierls bound. The framework could attempt to redefine $\phi$ as a free-energy landscape rather than an energy landscape, making the entropic selection a "gradient descent in free energy." But this is precisely the post-hoc redefinition the model already flagged as rendering the gradient claim unfalsifiable (Current Uncertainties, item 1). More importantly, at infinite temperature ($\beta = 0$), the free energy $F = E - TS$ is entirely dominated by $-TS$, so the "gradient" is purely entropic with zero energetic contribution — the framework's paradigmatic examples (gravity as mass rolling down curvature, electromagnetism as charge rolling down potential) are all *energetic* gradients, and the claim that "the equation is the same; the field changes" becomes vacuous when the "field" is an entropy landscape with no energetic content. The four structural conditions on the interaction $f$ that enable the ordering are combinatorial/analytic properties (subexponential growth, log-concavity-like bounds) that the framework has no mechanism to express. The Pirogov-Sinai / Peierls machinery providing the proof is a structural mathematical framework for competing ground-state phases that the five primitives cannot access.

**Paper 3: "Observation of Vinen turbulence during far-from-equilibrium Bose-Einstein condensation"**
ArXiv ID: 2604.28191v1. Selection type: validation.

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper concerns the Attractor primitive (the BEC ground state as the attractor basin), the Field primitive (turbulent dynamics as gradient-driven motion in the superfluid order parameter), and Resolution (the emergence of long-range order as the system resolves into coherence). The framework would narrate: a far-from-equilibrium quantum gas, initially in a disordered state, relaxes toward the BEC attractor. The relaxation pathway passes through a turbulent intermediate state — a tangle of quantized vortex lines (topological defects in the superfluid order parameter). These vortices are topological attractors (T042-type) whose decay follows Vinen's prediction $\mathcal{L} \propto t^{-1}$, corresponding to ultraquantum turbulence where vortex reconnection drives the decay. The key experimental findings — that the decay rate is independent of interaction strength and that weakly interacting compressible gases behave like incompressible hydrodynamic fluids at large scales — are consistent with the framework's Attractor primitive: the universality of the decay suggests the attractor basin's shape (the BEC ground state topology) determines the relaxation pathway independently of microscopic details. The vortex line-length density $\mathcal{L}$ measures how far the system is from the attractor, and its universal decay confirms that the attractor's basin structure dominates over microscopic dynamics. However, this is post-hoc consistency with zero differential predictive content. The framework could not have predicted: (a) the specific $t^{-1}$ decay law (this follows from Vinen's turbulence theory, not from any framework primitive); (b) the interaction-strength independence (this follows from the incompressible limit of superfluid hydrodynamics); (c) the matter-wave magnification technique or the density-slice imaging method; (d) the crossover between compressible and incompressible behavior. The framework's qualitative narrative — "disordered state relaxes through topological defect decay toward the ordered attractor" — is correct but could describe any phase-ordering system. The specific physics (quantized vortices, Vinen vs. Kolmogorov turbulence regimes, sound-speed-dependent compressibility crossover) is derived entirely from superfluid hydrodynamics and BEC physics. The framework adds vocabulary but no predictive content. This is the weakest validation category: correct qualitative narration shared with any theory that includes attractor dynamics and topological defects.

### FRAMEWORK STRAINS

The most severe strain from this cycle comes from **Paper 2** (order by disorder at arbitrarily high temperature). The strain is not merely another instance of the gradient equation's insufficiency — those are catalogued extensively (T006, T029, T070, T078, T098, T099, T101, T108, T109). This is a *foundational challenge to the Attractor primitive itself*. The Attractor primitive's definition — "local minima in potential fields are attractors" — is falsified as a general characterization of stable ordered states. The checkerboard order in Paper 2 is:

1. **Not an energy minimum.** The ordered configurations are not the lowest-energy states.
2. **Not produced by gradient descent** in any energetic potential.
3. **Stable at all temperatures, including infinite temperature**, where all energetic considerations vanish identically.
4. **Selected by entropy alone** — a counting/combinatorial mechanism, not a dynamical one.
5. **Proven rigorously** with Pirogov-Sinai theory, not conjectured or numerically suggested.

The framework could attempt the standard escape: redefine $\phi$ as the free energy $F = E - TS$ and claim the system descends the free-energy gradient. But this escape fails at $\beta = 0$ (infinite temperature), where $F = -TS$ and the "gradient" has zero energetic content. The framework's paradigmatic examples — gravity, electromagnetism, fitness landscapes — are all energetic. An "entropic gradient" at infinite temperature, where the system has uniform Boltzmann weight and all ordering comes from the multiplicity of configurations, is not what the framework's equation $\dot{x} = -\nabla\phi(x)$ was designed to describe. The ordering mechanism is the partial trace over occupation numbers selecting checkerboard configurations — a combinatorial/information-theoretic selection mechanism, not a dynamical one. This connects to and deepens T059 (ontological inversion): the stable state is selected by *combinatorial structure* (the four conditions on the interaction function $f$), not by dynamical evolution.

The strain from **Paper 1** is more surgical but equally important: it demonstrates that the framework's two most fundamental conservation and attractor primitives are in *quantitative tension with each other* at the black hole horizon. Conservation (unitarity) demands information preservation; the Attractor boundary (smooth horizon) demands information passage. These cannot both be exact. The trade-off inequality $\varepsilon \geq D_{\max}^2/8$ is a quantitative relationship between primitive-level concepts that the framework's qualitative architecture cannot express, and the resolution (pre-existing entanglement) requires information-theoretic structure outside any primitive.

**T134 — Entropic order at infinite temperature: stable ordered states can be selected by combinatorial/entropic mechanisms with zero energetic content, falsifying the Attractor primitive's energy-minimum definition**

Description: Mehta (2604.28026) rigorously proves that a class of classical lattice models on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ ($d \geq 2$) exhibits long-range checkerboard order at *all* temperatures, including the $\beta \to 0$ limit, where the ordered configurations are not energy minimizers. The ordering mechanism is purely entropic: checkerboard configurations are selected by the partial trace over occupation numbers because they maximize the available microstates. The four structural conditions on the interaction function $f$ (defined on $\mathbb{N}_0 \times \mathbb{N}_0$) that enable this ordering are combinatorial/analytic properties the framework cannot express. The proof uses Pirogov-Sinai theory with a Peierls bound — rigorous mathematical machinery for competing ground-state phases.

The framework's Attractor primitive defines attractors as "local minima in potential fields." The Field primitive says $\dot{x} = -\nabla\phi(x)$. Both presuppose that stable states are reached by energetic gradient descent. Paper 2 falsifies this: (a) the ordered state is not an energy minimum; (b) it is not reached by gradient descent in any energetic potential; (c) at $\beta = 0$ the energy landscape is flat — there are no energetic gradients at all — yet the system is ordered; (d) the selection mechanism is combinatorial (multiplicity of microstates), not dynamical. The standard escape — redefining $\phi$ as free energy $F = E - TS$ — fails at $\beta = 0$ where $F = -TS$ and the gradient is purely entropic. The framework's paradigmatic examples (gravity, electromagnetism) are energetic; a purely entropic "gradient" at infinite temperature, where the Boltzmann weight is uniform and all structure comes from the counting of configurations, is not captured by $\dot{x} = -\nabla\phi(x)$ in any non-trivial sense.

This is distinct from T006 (stable excited states from cyclic driving, which concerns non-equilibrium preparation of metastable states) because T134 concerns *equilibrium* order at *all temperatures* including infinite temperature. It is distinct from T061 (anti-gradient robustness, which concerns the gradient sign) because T134 concerns the *absence* of any energetic gradient. It is distinct from T126 (spectral unboundedness without pathology, which concerns non-energy conserved quantities defining the vacuum) because T134 concerns a system with a perfectly standard energy function where the ordered state is simply not the energy minimum. It is distinct from T023 (kinetic arrest, which concerns exponentially long-lived metastable states) because T134's ordered state is a true equilibrium state, not metastable.

Why unresolved: The framework would need to either (a) generalize the Attractor primitive from "energy minima" to "free-energy minima" and develop a theory of entropic gradients that is not trivially post-hoc — this requires specifying when entropic selection dominates and predicting which configurations are entropically selected, which requires importing the Pirogov-Sinai framework or its equivalent; (b) add a combinatorial/entropic selection primitive that operates alongside energetic gradient descent, specifying the conditions under which entropic ordering occurs — the four structural conditions on $f$ would be the natural starting point but they are combinatorial/analytic conditions with no expression in any current primitive; (c) acknowledge that the Attractor primitive's energy-minimum definition is a special case of a broader principle where the *relevant* extremization functional depends on the thermodynamic regime (energy-dominated at low $T$, entropy-dominated at high $T$, free-energy compromise at intermediate $T$) — but this makes the primitive dependent on temperature and thermodynamic context, fundamentally changing its character from a universal structural claim to a regime-dependent description. The infinite-temperature persistence is especially severe: it means there exists no temperature regime where the energetic Attractor primitive is the correct description of this system's ordering.
