### PAPERS PROCESSED

**Paper 1: Semi-Device-Independent Certification for Nonlocality without Entanglement**
ArXiv ID: 2606.13667v1 | Selection type: strain

Through the Worldlines lens, the Resolution primitive describes quantum measurement as the process by which unresolved states become definite, with the quantum advantage residing in the pre-resolution state's superposition and entanglement structure. Paper 1 directly contradicts this localization of nonclassicality. The authors demonstrate that global quantum measurements on ensembles of *fully separable* (zero-entanglement) states outperform separable measurements in maximum-confidence discrimination — establishing that the quantum advantage resides in the measurement structure itself, not in the state. The framework would narrate this as: "unresolved computational states become definite through resolution events," but the states here are already as "resolved" as they can be in terms of entanglement — they are product states with no inter-party quantum correlations. The nonclassical advantage arises from the *joint structure of the resolution process* (global POVM vs. LOCC), not from any property of the pre-resolution computation. The framework's implicit ontological assignment — nonclassicality lives in the pre-resolution state, resolution merely reveals it — is falsified. Furthermore, the paper reveals that "resolution" is not a single operation but a family of operationally distinct strategies (minimum-error, unambiguous, maximum-confidence), and the quantum/classical gap can appear at one operational level (confidence) while being absent at another (success probability). The Resolution primitive's undifferentiated "unresolved → definite" collapses this physically significant operational spectrum into a single concept. The semi-device-independent certification protocol is especially challenging: it treats the measurement *type* as the quantity to be certified from observed data, meaning the resolution process's own internal structure is experimentally accessible and physically meaningful — something the framework has no vocabulary for.

**Paper 2: One-loop five-point gluing analytically**
ArXiv ID: 2606.13665v1 | Selection type: strain

The framework would describe the one-loop five-point stress tensor correlator in $\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM as a computational output — the result of quantum field-theoretic computation propagating through ticks, with loop corrections representing additional virtual processes accumulated over additional computational steps. Paper 2 demonstrates that the actual evaluation of this observable proceeds through an entirely non-dynamical chain: integrability (algebraic spin chain structure) → hexagonalization/gluing (a decomposition of the correlator into form factors of integrable excitations) → residue series resummation (converting discrete sums into continuous Euler integrals) → intersection theory for generalized hypergeometric functions (a cohomological operation on moduli spaces of punctured spheres). At no point in this chain does any of the five primitives participate. The intersection numbers of twisted cohomology classes — which select the correct analytic continuations and produce the final integrated expressions — are mathematical objects from algebraic geometry that serve as the *computational engine* producing the physical answer. The framework's "reality is literally computation" competes directly with "reality is literally the cohomological structure of moduli spaces," with the latter providing the constructive evaluation while the former provides only post-hoc vocabulary. The five-level derivation depth (spin chain → form factors → residue series → Euler integrals → intersection theory) is the most technically complete demonstration yet that the framework's dynamical primitives are absent from the actual evaluation of fundamental physical observables. The fact that the analytic evaluation *required* intersection theory — no alternative purely dynamical route (e.g., direct Feynman diagram evaluation) produced the complete analytic answer at this complexity — means the structural/cohomological route is not merely a convenience but a necessity.
