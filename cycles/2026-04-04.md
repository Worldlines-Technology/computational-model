### PAPERS PROCESSED

**Paper 1: "Gravitational null rays: Covariant Quantization and the Dressing Time"**
ArXiv ID: 2604.02228v1 | Selection type: Strain

The framework claims reality updates in discrete ticks, with the speed of light as the tick rate, and that physical observables emerge through resolution of unresolved states. Paper 1 quantizes the degrees of freedom on a gravitational null ray — a structure that propagates at exactly the tick rate — using the gravitational field itself as a quantum reference frame (QRF). The framework has no account of what happens *on* the null ray, which is the tick's own propagation surface. The key result is that gauge-invariant observables form a Virasoro crossed product algebra, and that the "dressing time" — the internal clock constructed from gravity itself — is non-ideal: its coherent states have non-vanishing overlaps governed by the Kähler potential for Virasoro coadjoint orbits. In framework terms, this means: (a) the "tick" cannot serve as a clean reference frame because the gravitational field that defines it has irreducible quantum fuzziness — the dressing time's coherent states overlap, so the tick never fully resolves into a definite classical parameter; (b) the algebra of physical observables is not a simple operator algebra but carries Virasoro structure — an infinite-dimensional symmetry algebra that the framework's five primitives cannot express; (c) the "covariant normal ordering" prescription is a renormalization scheme that is background-independent but QRF-dependent, meaning the separation between "substrate" and "computation" depends on which quantum reference frame is chosen, which is itself part of the computation. The framework's Tick primitive assumes a clean, classical discretization parameter; this paper shows that at the quantum level, the tick is fuzzy, algebraically structured, and self-referential (the gravitational field provides its own clock). The Resolution primitive assumes states become "definite," but the non-ideal character of the dressing time means the temporal reference itself never becomes fully definite — resolution is always relative to a non-ideal clock. The anomaly cancellation mechanism — requiring a classical deformation to eliminate spurious quantum degrees of freedom — further demonstrates that the boundary between classical and quantum (between substrate and computation, in the framework's language) is not fixed but must be dynamically adjusted. This paper strains the framework at its most fundamental point: the Tick is not a clean primitive but a quantum, algebraically structured, self-referential, non-ideal object.

**Paper 2: "Robust Correlation-Induced Localization Under Time-Reversal Symmetry Breaking"**
ArXiv ID: 2604.02321v1 | Selection type: Strain

The framework's Field primitive describes dynamics via gradient descent ẍ = −∇φ(x), and the Attractor primitive describes stable states as local minima in potential landscapes. Paper 2 demonstrates a localization-delocalization phase transition driven by the interplay between long-range correlated hopping structure and time-reversal symmetry breaking (TRSB). The algebraically localized states exist not because they sit in energy minima (they are eigenstates distributed across the spectrum) but because the *correlation structure* of the hopping amplitudes creates effective confinement. This confinement is robust against finite TRSB strength but collapses at a critical value, producing a sharp phase transition. The framework faces several strains: (a) the localized states are not attractors in any potential landscape — they are consequences of the spectral/correlation structure of the Hamiltonian, which the framework's gradient equation cannot distinguish from a system without correlations; (b) the phase transition is driven by a *symmetry parameter* (the argument of complex hopping amplitudes controlling TRSB strength), not by attractor landscape restructuring — there is no potential whose minima merge or split; (c) the dynamical phase diagram (subdiffusive → diffusive transport as TRSB increases) shows that the *transport universality class* changes continuously with a symmetry parameter, reinforcing T029 and T072 — the framework has no mechanism for graded qualitative change in transport driven by symmetry parameters; (d) the robustness of localization against finite TRSB is a consequence of the *correlation structure* being a stronger organizer than the symmetry, which inverts the framework's implicit hierarchy where symmetry (via conservation) is more fundamental than correlation structure. The fact that correlations can protect localization against symmetry breaking, but only up to a threshold, is a quantitative structural result that no primitive can express.

**Paper 3: "Fluid perturbations from expanding bubbles in first-order phase transitions"**
ArXiv ID: 2604.02240v1 | Selection type: Validation

The framework's Attractor primitive describes phase transitions as attractor landscape restructuring, and the Resolution primitive describes states becoming definite. Paper 3 studies the velocity power spectrum generated during a cosmological first-order phase transition — precisely the kind of landscape restructuring event the framework describes qualitatively. The paper finds that the spectral breaks in the velocity field are determined by the positions of *discontinuities* in the velocity profiles (shock fronts, deflagration fronts), not by the bubble size or sound shell thickness as previously assumed. In framework terms, the phase transition produces propagating resolution fronts (bubble walls) that create discontinuities in the fluid field, and the spectral structure of the resulting perturbations is determined by the geometry of these discontinuities. The framework's qualitative description — "attractor landscape restructuring produces resolution dynamics with characteristic features" — is consistent with this picture. The dependence of spectral breaks on the wall velocity approaching the Chapman-Jouguet speed (rather than the sound speed) is a quantitative result about resolution front dynamics that the framework's primitives cannot derive. The framework would describe the bubble expansion as "computation propagating at the local resolution speed through the field," which is vaguely consistent but adds no predictive content: the paper's results are derived entirely from relativistic hydrodynamics and the geometry of spherical shell profiles. The distinction between exponential and simultaneous nucleation distributions affecting the spectral template is a detail about the *statistics of resolution initiation* that the Resolution primitive (which says nothing about when or where resolution begins) cannot address. **Verdict: post-hoc consistency only.** The framework's vocabulary can be applied after the fact (bubble walls as resolution fronts, spectral breaks as signatures of discontinuities in the resolved field), but the framework contributed zero predictive content, zero quantitative constraints, and zero structural insights that were not already present in relativistic hydrodynamics. The specific finding that breaks correspond to discontinuity positions rather than geometric scales (bubble size, shell thickness) is a result the framework could not have anticipated, and it actually slightly undermines the Attractor primitive's emphasis on basin geometry (scale of the attractor) as the determinant of transition features.

### FRAMEWORK STRAINS

The most important strain this cycle comes from Paper 1. The framework's most fundamental primitive — the Tick — is revealed as deeply inadequate at the quantum gravitational level. The paper shows that when you actually quantize the degrees of freedom on a null ray (the Tick's own propagation surface), the result is:

1. **The tick is self-referential and non-ideal.** The gravitational field provides its own clock (the "dressing time"), but this clock's coherent states overlap — they are never fully distinguishable. The overlap is governed by the Teo-Takhtajan energy, which is the Kähler potential for Virasoro coadjoint orbits. This means the Tick never achieves the clean discreteness the framework assumes. The "discrete update step" is, at the quantum level, a fuzzy, continuously parameterized, algebraically structured object whose precision is governed by the Kähler geometry of an infinite-dimensional space (the Virasoro group's coadjoint orbits). The framework says "reality updates in discrete steps"; the paper says "the steps themselves are quantum-mechanical objects with irreducible fuzziness whose structure is governed by infinite-dimensional algebra."

2. **The observable algebra is not a simple collection of "resolved values" but a Virasoro crossed product.** The physical observables on a null ray carry the structure of an infinite-dimensional symmetry algebra. The Resolution primitive says states become "definite" — but definite *with respect to what*? The paper shows that the answer is "with respect to the dressing time QRF," and that this reference frame is itself part of the gravitational computation. The framework's clean separation between the tick (the update step) and the computation (what gets updated) collapses: the tick is part of the computation, and the computation determines the tick.

3. **The substrate/computation boundary is QRF-dependent.** The "covariant normal ordering" prescription — which determines what counts as vacuum fluctuation vs. physical excitation — depends on the choice of quantum reference frame. Different QRF choices produce different decompositions of the same physics into "substrate" (vacuum) and "computation" (excitation). The framework treats the substrate as a fixed, observer-independent ground; the paper shows it is QRF-dependent and therefore not a fixed ontological category.

This is not a peripheral strain. It strikes at the framework's most basic primitive and shows that the Tick, when properly quantized using gravity's own degrees of freedom, is a quantum, self-referential, algebraically rich, non-ideal object that cannot be described as a "discrete update step." The framework's foundational metaphor — reality as a computer with a clock rate — breaks down precisely where it should be most literally applicable: on the null ray where the clock propagates.
