**Paper 1: "Beyond Discontinuities: Cosmological WFCs from the Supersymmetric Orthogonal Grassmannian"**
ArXiv ID: 2604.08512v1 | Selection type: Strain

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper describes a situation where the "computation" of cosmological wavefunction coefficients (WFCs) for conserved currents cannot be completed by a single geometric object (the orthogonal Grassmannian). The Grassmannian provides only discontinuities — partial computational output — and requires augmentation by a supersymmetric kinematic prefactor derived from $\mathcal{N}=2$ relations between spinning and non-spinning sectors. The framework would need to interpret this as: the computational substrate produces incomplete results via one channel (geometric/Grassmannian) and requires a structurally distinct algebraic operation (SUSY completion) to yield physically complete answers. But this "completion" is not a dynamical process — it is not a tick, not gradient descent, not resolution, not conservation. It is a structural relation between different sectors of the theory that provides missing information. The positive and negative branches of the Grassmannian producing distinct flat-space helicity amplitudes means the geometric sign structure encodes physically distinguishable outcomes, determined by geometry and algebra rather than by any dynamical primitive. The framework strains severely: the physical content is co-determined by two mathematical structures (Grassmannian geometry and SUSY algebra) neither of which is expressible in the five primitives, and their *combination* — the specific way SUSY completes the Grassmannian — is a structural operation the framework has no vocabulary for. This paper was already anticipated by T104 in the open tensions, but this cycle's processing confirms and sharpens the strain.

**Paper 2: "On Carrollian Loop Amplitudes for Gauge Theory and Gravity"**

The framework's Tick primitive generates null infinity but has no account of the Carrollian conformal structure, the differential operator hierarchy, or the descendant relations that organize the computation at its own boundary. The framework describes the journey (propagation at the tick rate) but is constitutively silent about the destination's structure.
