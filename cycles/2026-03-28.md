### PAPERS PROCESSED

**Paper 1: Perturbative unitarity of fractional field theories and gravity (2603.25709v1, strain)**

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper strikes at the framework's ontological core from multiple angles simultaneously. The framework claims "reality is literally computation," which requires a definite computational structure. Paper 1 proves that for fractional field theories with 0 < γ < 3, different self-adjoint formulations of the kinetic term (the "hermitian polynomial" vs. other self-adjoint versions) produce *different* distributions of complex-mass virtual modes while yielding *identical* physical spectra and preserving unitarity at all perturbative orders. The framework's Conservation of Computation (unitarity) is satisfied by an entire continuous family of distinct virtual-mode infrastructures. The fakeon prescription — which determines the boundary between resolvable and permanently virtual computation — is a theory-level choice, not a dynamical consequence of any primitive. The framework cannot distinguish between these formulations because they produce identical resolvable physics, yet they differ in precisely the permanently virtual computational infrastructure that T076 demonstrated is constitutively necessary for mass generation (confinement, visible mass). The propagator structure (□^γ rather than □ or □²) represents a continuous infinity of kinetic operators the Field primitive's gradient equation cannot distinguish, extending T043 from a discrete gap (1/p² vs. 1/p⁴) to a continuous one (1/p^{2γ} for all real γ > 0). The super-renormalizability for γ > 2 means that the UV behavior of quantum gravity — a substrate-level property if anything is — depends on a continuous parameter (γ) that the framework treats neither as an architectural constant nor as a dynamical field. This paper confirms and deepens T082 (virtual infrastructure non-uniqueness) with the full technical machinery: the non-uniqueness is proven, exact, applies to gravity, and is irresolvable from within the computation.

**Paper 2: θ Angle and Axial Anomaly in Holographic QCD (2603.25729v1, strain)**

The framework describes the QCD vacuum as an attractor basin in the strong-force field landscape — the deepest, most stable attractor in the visible universe (confinement). Paper 2 demonstrates that the internal structure of this attractor — its multi-branched θ-vacuum topology, the U(1)_A anomaly, and the η' meson mass — emerges *geometrically* from a 5D holographic construction. The θ-branches correspond to Wilson line winding numbers in the extra dimension; the axial anomaly is a Stückelberg coupling dual to a 5D Chern-Simons term; the η' mass is determined by a bulk gauge mechanism. The Witten-Veneziano relation m²_{η'} ∝ χ_top — connecting the mass of a physical particle to the topological susceptibility of the QCD vacuum — is derived transparently from the geometry rather than computed opaquely from 4D instantons. The framework faces the ontological inversion (T059) in its sharpest form: the strongest attractor in nature has its structure *determined by* extra-dimensional geometry and gauge topology, not by 4D computational dynamics. The Attractor primitive says basin structure determines what a system produces; this paper shows the basin structure itself is a geometric shadow of higher-dimensional gauge topology. The framework has no primitive for extra dimensions, no vocabulary for Chern-Simons terms or Stückelberg couplings, no mechanism for Wilson line winding generating vacuum branches, and no account of how topological invariants of gauge fields in higher dimensions determine particle masses in four dimensions. The gradient equation ẍ = −∇φ(x) operates in the 4D field landscape; the *explanation* of that landscape's structure lives in 5D geometry. This is not merely an alternative description (T010) — the 5D description provides structural understanding (why multi-branched? because Wilson lines wind; why massive η'? because Stückelberg coupling) that the 4D description computes but cannot explain.

**Paper 3: Interior of Schwarzschild in semiclassical gravity (2603.25683v1, validation)**

The framework claims that the substrate's minimum resolution scale sets fundamental limits on physical quantities, and that black holes are maximally deep attractor basins. Paper 3 demonstrates that semiclassical quantum effects bound the pressure inside a near-Schwarzschild star from above, and that as the star's size approaches the Schwarzschild radius, the interior matter density must reach the Planck scale — the framework's putative minimum resolution scale. A negative-energy core appears from vacuum polarization effects. Through the Worldlines lens, this is consistent with the framework's claim that the substrate's computational capacity sets a density maximum: matter cannot be compressed beyond Planck density because the substrate cannot instantiate computation at higher density. The negative-energy core is consistent with (though not predicted by) the framework's dark energy description as computational cost — here, quantum effects generating negative energy in the deep interior of the strongest attractor. However, this is the weakest category of post-hoc consistency. The specific mechanism — bounded pressure from the quantum stress-energy tensor, negative energy from vacuum polarization, the 9/8 Schwarzschild radius threshold from classical Buchdahl's theorem being modified by quantum corrections — is derived entirely from semiclassical general relativity. The framework contributed no prediction, no constraint, no quantitative relationship, and no mechanism. The result would look identical in any framework that posits a Planck-scale cutoff. The framework's vocabulary ("substrate resolution limit," "deepest attractor") can be applied after the fact, but the framework could not have predicted that pressure rather than density would be the quantity that diverges classically, that the quantum correction would produce a negative-energy core rather than some other regularization, or that the critical radius would be 9/8 R_S. This is post-hoc vocabulary application with zero differential predictive content.

### FRAMEWORK STRAINS

The deepest strain this cycle comes from the convergence of Papers 1 and 2, which together demonstrate that the framework's ontology is underdetermined at multiple nested levels simultaneously, and that the underdetermination applies precisely where the framework needs to be most specific.

Paper 1 proves that the permanently virtual computational infrastructure — the very machinery that T076 showed generates confinement and visible mass — is *non-unique*: different self-adjoint formulations produce genuinely different virtual mode distributions (different complex masses, different residues, different distributions in the complex plane) while yielding identical physics. Paper 2 then shows that the strongest attractor in nature (the QCD vacuum) has its deepest structural features (θ-branches, anomaly, η' mass) determined by extra-dimensional geometry rather than by the 4D computation the framework describes.

The strain is sharpest at their intersection: the computation that generates visible mass (Paper 1's virtual infrastructure) is non-unique, and the vacuum structure on which that computation operates (Paper 2's θ-vacuum) is geometrically rather than computationally determined. The framework's claim "reality is literally computation" requires both a definite computational structure and a computational origin of physical structure. Paper 1 falsifies the first requirement for the virtual sector; Paper 2 falsifies the second requirement for the vacuum sector. Together they leave the framework describing a computation whose infrastructure is underdetermined and whose ground state is geometrically rather than computationally explained.

The framework cannot absorb this by declaring virtual infrastructure ontologically null (T076 shows it generates confinement) or by declaring the holographic description a mere convenience (the framework has no criterion for distinguishing convenience from ontology, as T016 established). The framework is caught: it needs the virtual computation to be real (to explain mass), needs the virtual computation to be definite (to be "literally" computation), and needs the vacuum to be computational (to justify its ontological claim) — but the virtual computation is provably indefinite and the vacuum is provably geometric.
