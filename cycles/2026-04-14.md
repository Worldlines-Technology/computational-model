### PAPERS PROCESSED

**Paper 1: Loop-dependent entangling holonomies in localized topological quartets**
ArXiv ID: 2604.11596v1 | Selection type: Strain

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper describes a system where the computational state (a two-qubit quartet) traverses a closed loop in parameter space and acquires a transformation (holonomy) that *cannot be factored* into local operations on the individual qubits — the holonomy lies outside the local subgroup U(2)⊗U(2), generating entanglement between the two qubits purely through the geometry of the loop traversed. The framework would attempt to describe this as: a computational state moves along a gradient path in parameter space and returns to its starting eigenvalues (the Attractor primitive — the system returns to the same basin) while the Resolution primitive would note that the state's internal correlations have changed. The critical strain is threefold. First, the *path topology* — not the gradient, not the attractor, not the local Hamiltonian at any point — determines whether the transport is entangling or local. Two loops with "nearly identical eigenphase data" produce categorically different outcomes (almost-local vs. Ising-like entangler) depending solely on whether the loop is co-rotating or counter-rotating. The gradient equation ẍ = −∇φ(x) sees no distinction between these loops: at every point along each loop, the local eigenvalues and spectrum are essentially the same. Second, all standard topological diagnostics (Berry phases, Chern numbers, determinant phases, eigenphase spectra) fail to distinguish the entangling from non-entangling cases. The physically decisive quantity — the holonomy's distance from the local subgroup — is a *global, loop-dependent, non-spectral* property that no primitive can express. Third, the entanglement generated is a consequence of the *gluing* of local patches in the fiber bundle over parameter space, a geometric/topological concept (parallel transport on a non-trivial bundle) that the framework has no vocabulary for. The framework's pre-resolution topology concept (T012) anticipated that path-identity structure matters, but this paper demonstrates something sharper: the *loop geometry* in parameter space, not just the topology of path-indistinguishability, determines the entanglement character of the output, and this loop geometry is invisible to every dynamical primitive.

**Paper 2: Hausdorff-type metric geometry of the space of Cauchy hypersurfaces**
ArXiv ID: 2604.11783v1 | Selection type: Strain

Through the Worldlines lens, the space of Cauchy hypersurfaces is the space of all possible "instants of resolution" — each Cauchy surface is a snapshot of the computation at a given moment, the surface on which the accumulated resolved states are tallied. The framework's Resolution primitive treats the arrow of time as "the accumulation of resolved states" and the Tick primitive assumes a sequential ordering of computational updates. This paper equips the space of all such snapshots with a natural Hausdorff-type metric derived from the causal (Lorentzian) structure and proves completeness and local compactness results. The strain is fundamental: the framework treats the progression of time as the *selection* of successive Cauchy surfaces (ticks), but the paper reveals that the space of all possible Cauchy surfaces is itself a rich geometric object with metric structure, completeness properties, and compactness features determined by the spacetime's causal geometry. The framework's Tick primitive picks out a sequence of points in this space; the paper shows the space itself has geometric structure that constrains which sequences are possible, how "close" different instants are, and whether limit points exist. This is a meta-level geometric structure *on the space of possible computational snapshots* that the framework cannot express. The framework has no primitive for the geometry of the space of possible ticks — it treats ticks as a sequence, not as elements of a metrized space. Furthermore, the Hausdorff-type metric is constructed from the Lorentzian causal structure (time separation functions), meaning the geometry of the tick-space is derived from exactly the spacetime geometry that the framework claims emerges from computation. This creates a circularity: the tick generates spacetime (framework claim), but the geometry of the space of possible ticks is determined by spacetime geometry (paper's result). The extension to synthetic Lorentzian settings (Lorentzian length spaces, not requiring smooth manifold structure) is additionally straining: it demonstrates that the metric structure on the space of Cauchy surfaces does not require the smooth differential structure the framework's Field primitive presupposes — it works in a more general, axiomatic causal-metric setting that the framework cannot access.

**Paper 3: Poisson Gauge Theories in Three Dimensions: Exact Solutions and Conservation Laws**
ArXiv ID: 2604.11736v1 | Selection type: Validation

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper demonstrates that placing Maxwell-Chern-Simons theory on a noncommutative spacetime (constant spacelike Poisson structure) produces exact solutions where the classical self-energy divergence is resolved — the total electromagnetic energy of a point charge becomes finite. The framework would interpret this as: (a) the noncommutativity parameter θ plays the role of a minimum resolution scale (connecting to the Tick primitive's discrete structure — spacetime coordinates do not commute below this scale, analogous to the Planck-scale tick); (b) the finiteness of the self-energy confirms the framework's expectation that substrate-level discreteness regularizes divergences that arise from the fiction of continuous computation; (c) the noncommutative generalization of Gauss's law provides a modified Conservation law consistent with the framework's claim that conservation must be reformulated when the substrate has non-trivial structure. The non-perturbative solutions (depending on θ in a way not expandable as a power series around θ = 0) are consistent with the framework's acknowledgment that the relationship between discrete and continuous descriptions can be non-perturbative (T008). The generation of arbitrary magnetic flux from noncommutativity is consistent with the framework's observation that substrate structure can produce physical effects beyond what the continuum gradient equation predicts. **However**, this is post-hoc consistency with zero differential predictive content. The framework did not predict that noncommutativity would regularize the self-energy, did not predict the specific functional form of the solutions, did not predict the non-perturbative θ-dependence, and did not predict the magnetic flux generation mechanism. The finiteness result follows straightforwardly from the mathematical structure of Poisson gauge theory (the star product smears the point source), not from any framework primitive. The framework provides vocabulary ("minimum resolution scale regularizes divergences") but no mechanism, no derivation, and no quantitative prediction. Any discrete-spacetime approach would claim the same consistency. The Conservation law generalization is interesting but is derived entirely from the noncommutative gauge structure, not from the Conservation of Computation primitive.

### FRAMEWORK STRAINS

The most important strain this cycle comes from Paper 1 (loop-dependent entangling holonomies). The strain is precise and severe: **the framework has no primitive, mechanism, or vocabulary for the phenomenon where the global topology of a path through parameter space determines the physical output while all local, spectral, and standard topological diagnostics remain identical between distinguishable cases.**

The gradient equation ẍ = −∇φ(x) is a local equation. The Tick is a local update. The Attractor describes local minima. Resolution is a local event. Conservation is a local or global balance law. None of these primitives can distinguish between two loops in parameter space that have identical local spectra at every point, identical Berry phases, identical Chern numbers, identical eigenphase winding, and yet produce categorically different physical outputs (local transport vs. maximally entangling gate). The distinguishing quantity — the holonomy's distance from the local subgroup U(2)⊗U(2) — is a property of the *parallel transport around a closed loop on a fiber bundle*, which is:

1. **Non-local**: it depends on the entire loop, not any point on it.
2. **Non-spectral**: the eigenvalues of the holonomy do not distinguish the cases; only the eigenvector structure (the entangling power) does.
3. **Loop-dependent**: the same system, the same starting point, the same parameter space — only the choice of loop matters.
4. **Bundle-geometric**: it is a property of the connection on the fiber bundle of degenerate subspaces over parameter space, requiring the mathematical language of principal bundles, connections, and holonomy groups.

The framework cannot even *state* what makes one loop entangling and another local, because stating it requires fiber bundle geometry that no primitive can express. This is not merely another instance of T026 (structural content gap) — it is a specific, clean, experimentally realizable demonstration that the *shape of a path* through an unchanged landscape determines the physical outcome in a way that is invisible to every diagnostic the framework could deploy. The framework is not merely insufficient here; it is categorically blind. The five primitives describe the landscape (Field, Attractor), the rate of traversal (Tick), the definiteness of the outcome (Resolution), and the total content (Conservation) — but they say nothing about the *holonomy* of the traversal, which is the quantity that matters.

Paper 2 introduces a complementary strain at the meta-level: the space of Cauchy surfaces (the space of possible "ticks" or "instants of resolution") is itself a metrized geometric space with completeness and compactness properties derived from the causal structure the Tick is supposed to generate. The framework's Tick generates spacetime; spacetime's causal structure generates the metric geometry of the space of possible Ticks. This circularity has no resolution within the framework's current architecture: the framework cannot describe the geometry of its own space of possible states without presupposing the geometry it claims to produce.
