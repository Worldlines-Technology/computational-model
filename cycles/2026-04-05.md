The framework's Tick primitive asserts "the universe updates in discrete steps" with the speed of light as the tick rate. The Page-Wootters reduction to the dressing time perspective shows that the classical tick — the framework's most basic primitive — is an emergent approximation from a deeper quantum gravitational structure. The framework is not merely incomplete here; its foundational primitive is self-undermining. The "discrete step" is a coarse-grained description of something that, at the fundamental level, is continuous, fuzzy, self-referential, and algebraically structured in ways the framework cannot express.

**Paper 2: "Localized formation of quiescent big bang singularities"**
ArXiv ID: 2604.02104v1 | Selection type: Strain

The framework treats singularities ambiguously — they are either substrate breakdowns or field-specific computational pathologies (T040, T044). Paper 2 proves that quiescent big bang singularities form *locally* from generic initial data satisfying a general condition, without proximity to any background solution. The singularity forms to the past of a specified point, curvature blows up, and yet the solution induces well-defined *geometric initial data on the singularity itself*. This is a sharp challenge: the framework's implicit picture of singularities as points where computation terminates (the substrate breaks) is contradicted by a singularity that carries structured, well-defined geometric data. The singularity is not an absence of information but a surface with its own geometry — a boundary condition on the computation, not a termination of it. The localization result is additionally important: the singularity forms from local initial data on an open set, meaning it is not a global catastrophe but a locally determined phenomenon. The framework's Attractor primitive might describe this as "the gravitational attractor captures nearby initial data," but the attractor here is a singularity — a point where curvature diverges — and the framework has no account of singularities that are simultaneously computational endpoints (curvature blows up) and geometric data carriers (the singularity has induced geometry). The matter-model independence of the formulation (the new time function and symmetric hyperbolic system work for any matter content) suggests the singularity formation is a structural property of Einstein's equations, not a dynamical consequence of specific fields — reinforcing T059 (ontological inversion). The synchronization of the singularity via a time function satisfying a second-order ODE is itself a structural/geometric operation the framework cannot express.

This is the weakest category of consistency — vocabulary application with zero differential content.
