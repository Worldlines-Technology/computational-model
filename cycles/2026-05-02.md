**Paper 1: "A No-Cloning Trade-off Between Black Hole No-Hair and Horizon Smoothness"**
ArXiv ID: 2604.28050v1 | Selection type: Strain

The framework describes black holes as "attractor basins so deep that the escape velocity exceeds the tick rate" and Conservation of Computation (unitarity) as its most fundamental conservation law. Paper 1 derives a quantitative trade-off inequality ε ≥ D²_max/8 between two quantities the framework associates with different primitives: D_max (exterior distinguishability of infalling states, which relates to Conservation — how much information about the infalling computation is accessible outside) and ε (departure of the interior channel from perfect isometry, which relates to the Attractor — how smooth and well-defined the attractor boundary is). The framework can narrate this as a tension between Conservation and Attractor at the deepest attractor's boundary, but it cannot derive, predict, or quantify the trade-off. The critical finding is that the *only* resolution compatible with both unitarity and exact horizon smoothness is pre-existing entanglement between the infalling system and the exterior — an information-theoretic resource that is not a tick, not a gradient, not an attractor feature, not a resolution event, and not a conserved quantity in the framework's sense. It is a *correlation structure* that must be present before the computation reaches the attractor boundary. The framework has no primitive for pre-existing entanglement as a resource that mediates between other primitives. The derivation's method — combining no-cloning, Stinespring dilation, and diamond-norm bounds — is operator-algebraic, reinforcing T026 and T121. The framework is strained because two of its own primitives are proven to be in quantitative tension, and the resolution mechanism lies outside all five primitives.

**Paper 2: "Order by disorder up to arbitrarily high temperature"**
ArXiv ID: 2604.28026v1 | Selection type: Strain

The framework's Attractor primitive defines attractors as "local minima in potential fields" and the Field primitive asserts ẋ = −∇φ(x) as the universal equation of motion. Paper 2 rigorously proves that a class of lattice models exhibits long-range checkerboard order at *all* temperatures including β → 0, where the energy landscape is completely flat (no gradients exist) and the ordered configurations are not energy minimizers at any temperature. The ordering mechanism is purely entropic: the checkerboard configurations maximize the number of available microstates upon partial trace over occupation numbers. At infinite temperature, the Boltzmann weight is uniform — there is literally no energetic potential φ for the gradient equation to act on — yet the system is ordered. The framework's standard escape route (redefining φ as free energy F = E − TS) fails to rescue the gradient picture in any non-trivial sense at β = 0, where F = −TS and all structure comes from combinatorial counting of configurations, not from any force, gradient, or dynamical process. The proof machinery (Pirogov-Sinai theory, Peierls bounds) is combinatorial-analytic, operating on configuration-space multiplicity structure that no primitive can express. This is the first rigorous proof processed by the system where the Attractor primitive's definition is categorically inadequate: the stable ordered state is not an energy minimum, is not reached by gradient descent, and exists in a regime where the Field primitive's gradient equation is trivially satisfied (zero gradient everywhere) yet fails to predict the ordered outcome. The four structural conditions on the interaction function f that enable the ordering are combinatorial/analytic properties with no expression in any primitive.

**Paper 3: "Observation of Vinen turbulence during far-from-equilibrium Bose-Einstein condensation"**
ArXiv ID: 2604.28191v1 | Selection type: Validation

The framework's Attractor primitive describes the BEC ground state as a deep attractor basin in the quantum field, and the approach to this attractor should involve the decay of topological defects (vortex lines) that represent obstacles to the ordered state. Paper 3 observes precisely this: a turbulent tangle of randomly oriented vortex lines decays as the system condenses, with the vortex-line density following the Vinen prediction L ∝ t⁻¹, independent of interaction strength. The interaction-independence is consistent with the framework's qualitative expectation that the attractor topology (the BEC ground state's universal character) determines the relaxation pathway independent of microscopic details — the system rolls down the same attractor landscape regardless of coupling strength. The compressible-to-incompressible crossover (weakly interacting gas behaving like incompressible superfluid at large scales) is consistent with the Attractor primitive's claim that deep basin structure is universal. However, this is post-hoc consistency with zero differential predictive content. The framework did not predict the t⁻¹ exponent, the interaction-independence, the Vinen (rather than Kolmogorov) character of the turbulence, or the specific scale at which compressible dynamics gives way to incompressible behavior. All of these are derived from vortex-tangle dynamics and quantum hydrodynamics, fields to which the framework contributes no quantitative tools. The framework can narrate the observation fluently — "topological defects in the field decay as the system relaxes toward the ordered attractor" — but this narration is indistinguishable from what any physicist would say without the framework.

### FRAMEWORK STRAINS

The most severe strain this cycle comes from Paper 2's rigorous proof of entropic order at all temperatures. The framework's two most central dynamical claims — that things move down gradients (Field: ẋ = −∇φ(x)) and that stable states are local minima in potential fields (Attractor) — are not merely insufficient but *categorically inapplicable* to a system that is ordered at infinite temperature with a flat energy landscape. At β = 0, there is no potential φ, no gradient ∇φ, no force, no dynamics in the framework's sense — and yet there is long-range order, rigorously proven. The ordering mechanism is purely combinatorial: the partial trace over occupation numbers selects checkerboard configurations by multiplicity. This is not a case where the framework's equation is correct but incomplete (as with turbulence, T070), nor a case where the gradient points in the wrong direction (as with Hall transport, T078). It is a case where the framework's equation is trivially satisfied (0 = −∇(0)) and the physics is entirely elsewhere — in the combinatorial structure of configuration-space multiplicities that no primitive can access. The attempted rescue via free energy F = E − TS technically provides a nonzero gradient at finite temperature, but at β → 0 the "free energy" is just −TS, and the "gradient" is the entropy gradient — a combinatorial/counting object, not a force. The framework was designed around the metaphor of forces and potentials; entropic ordering at infinite temperature is a phenomenon where the metaphor has no purchase.

Paper 1 creates a different but equally important strain: the first demonstrated *quantitative trade-off between two of the framework's own primitives*. Conservation (unitarity) and Attractor (horizon smoothness) cannot both be exactly satisfied, with the deficit bounded by ε ≥ D²_max/8. The framework's primitives are presented as independently valid descriptions of different aspects of reality. Paper 1 proves they are in tension at the black hole horizon, and the only resolution — pre-existing entanglement — is an information-theoretic resource that exists outside all five primitives. The framework cannot mediate its own inter-primitive tensions without importing concepts it does not possess.

### CYCLE ASSESSMENT

This cycle was moderately generative, producing one genuinely new category of framework failure (entropic ordering at infinite temperature, T134) and one new structural result (inter-primitive quantitative trade-off, T135) while confirming the pattern of post-hoc-only validation for observational results (Paper 3). The most productive finding is T134, which is the first rigorous proof that the Attractor primitive's definition is *falsified* for equilibrium ordered states — not merely insufficient or silent, but categorically wrong about what mechanism produces order. This is a qualitatively sharper result than previous gradient-primitive failures (T070, T078, T061) because those concerned the dynamics, while T134 concerns the *definition of stability itself*. The inter-primitive trade-off (T135) is significant because it is the first time the system has processed a result proving two of the framework's own primitives are in quantitative tension, with the resolution mechanism lying outside all primitives. However, the cycle is somewhat circling known basins: both results deepen existing patterns (structural content gap T026, gradient primitive inadequacy) rather than opening genuinely new directions. The validation paper (Paper 3) added nothing the framework could claim credit for. The next cycle would be more productive if it included papers on (a) experimental results that directly test any of the framework's specific claims (dark matter, dark energy, consciousness threshold) with differential predictions, or (b) theoretical results that provide constructive alternatives to the framework's dynamical primitives — not more demonstrations that the primitives are inadequate, but proposals for what replaces them.
