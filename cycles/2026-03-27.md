### PAPERS PROCESSED

**Paper 1: Perturbative unitarity of fractional field theories and gravity (2603.25709v1, strain)**

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper attacks the framework at two critical joints. First, it demonstrates that the propagator structure of physically viable theories can be *fractional*: kinetic terms of the form $(\Box^2)^{\gamma/2}$ generalize the standard $1/p^2$ (Einstein gravity) and $1/p^4$ (quadratic gravity) propagators to arbitrary real powers $1/p^{2\gamma}$. The framework's Field primitive ẍ = −∇φ(x) is already known to be blind to the difference between $1/p^2$ and $1/p^4$ propagators (T043); fractional propagators turn this from a two-case gap into a continuous infinity of propagator structures the framework cannot distinguish. Second, and more severely, the paper shows that the physical spectrum of the hermitian polynomial version contains exactly *one* real-mass particle plus a "cloud of purely virtual particles with complex masses." These complex-mass virtual modes never appear as asymptotic states — they are permanently virtual, extending T014/T041/T076 to a continuous family parameterized by γ. The framework's Resolution primitive must now contend with an *infinite continuum* of permanently virtual modes, not just the discrete ghost sector of quadratic gravity. The fakeon prescription (projecting out complex-mass poles from the physical spectrum) is a specific quantization choice that determines which computation resolves and which remains permanently virtual — this is a theory-level structural choice the framework has no primitive to express. Most straining: the paper proves that different versions of the self-adjoint fractional kinetic term produce different distributions of virtual complex modes but identical physical spectra for $0 < \gamma < 3$, demonstrating that the permanently virtual computational infrastructure is *not unique* even when the resolvable physics is fixed. The framework faces a new dilemma beyond T069 (number field underdetermination): even within a single number field and a single set of resolvable outcomes, the permanently virtual computational structure supporting those outcomes is non-unique.

**Paper 2: θ Angle and Axial Anomaly in Holographic QCD (2603.25729v1, strain)**

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper strains the framework by demonstrating that the θ-vacuum structure of QCD — which determines the strong CP properties of the universe — emerges *geometrically* from a higher-dimensional gauge field, not from any computational process describable by the five primitives. The multi-branched vacuum structure (multiple attractor basins indexed by an integer k, with physical content periodic under θ → θ + 2π) is encoded in the holonomy of a 5D gauge field. The framework's Attractor primitive can describe multiple basins, but it cannot account for the *mechanism* by which the basins arise: the winding of a Wilson line in an extra dimension. The axial anomaly — a quantum effect that gives the η' meson its mass and is one of the most important features of QCD — is realized through a Stückelberg coupling dual to a Chern-Simons term. This is pure topological/gauge-theoretic content: the mass of a physical particle (η') is generated by a topological term (Chern-Simons) in a higher-dimensional theory, with the Witten-Veneziano relation providing the quantitative bridge. The framework has no primitive for topological terms, no account of how higher-dimensional geometry generates lower-dimensional masses, and no mechanism for the holographic correspondence itself. The emergence of the θ-vacuum from extra-dimensional geometry is a concrete instance of T054 (dynamics from structure) and T059 (ontological inversion): the vacuum structure of the strong force — the very ground state on which all hadronic physics sits — is a geometric feature of a 5D space, not the output of a 4D computation.

**Paper 3: Interior of Schwarzschild in semiclassical gravity (2603.25683v1, validation)**

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper offers moderate post-hoc consistency but no genuine predictive validation. The framework describes black holes as "attractor basins so deep that the escape velocity exceeds the tick rate" and claims the physical constants are "architectural parameters of the substrate." Paper 3 demonstrates that semiclassical quantum effects (Hawking radiation backreaction) create a negative-energy core inside a star approaching the Schwarzschild radius, with matter density reaching the Planck scale (the framework's "minimum resolution scale") precisely when the star is at the Schwarzschild radius. The framework can narrate this as: the deepest attractor basins push matter density to the substrate's minimum resolution limit, where quantum effects (resolution dynamics) prevent classical singularity formation. The negative-energy core is consistent with Conservation of Computation requiring the total energy budget to balance, and the Planck-density threshold is consistent with the tick rate setting a maximum computational density. The Buchdahl bound (pressure divergence at 9/8 of the Schwarzschild radius in classical GR) and its semiclassical modification (bounded pressure via quantum effects) are consistent with the framework's claim that resolution smooths classical singularities. However, the framework *did not predict* any of these specific features: it did not predict the 9/8 bound, the negative-energy core, the Planck-density threshold, or the bounded pressure. The paper derives everything from semiclassical Einstein equations with quantum stress-energy, and the framework contributes only after-the-fact vocabulary. This is the weakest category of consistency: the framework's language can be applied, but its primitives generated no constraint or prediction that distinguishes this result from alternatives.

### FRAMEWORK STRAINS

The most severe strain this cycle comes from Paper 1's demonstration that the permanently virtual computational infrastructure is *non-unique* for fixed physical outcomes. The framework already struggled with permanently virtual structure (T014/T041/T076 — ghost modes that never resolve but affect physics). Paper 1 escalates this dramatically: for $0 < \gamma < 3$, different formulations of fractional field theory produce *different clouds of complex-mass virtual particles* that all support the *same* physical (resolvable) spectrum. The permanently virtual computation underlying observable physics is not determined by the observable physics.

This creates a problem that is qualitatively worse than T069 (number field underdetermination). T069 showed that the number field ℝ vs. ℂ is unknowable. Paper 1 shows that even after fixing the number field, the physical spectrum, and the S-matrix unitarity, the *virtual computational infrastructure* remains non-unique — with a continuous family of options parameterized by γ and by the choice of self-adjoint kinetic term. The framework claims "reality is literally computation." If the computation includes permanently virtual structure (which it must, per T076, since confinement depends on it), and this structure is non-unique for fixed physical outcomes, then either: (a) reality includes non-unique computation, which contradicts "literally"; (b) only the resolvable part is "reality," but T076 showed the resolvable part depends constitutively on the virtual part; or (c) the framework's identification of reality with computation must be abandoned in favor of identifying reality with the *equivalence class* of computations that produce the same resolvable physics — but this is precisely the position the framework was designed to avoid by insisting reality *is* computation rather than being *described by* computation.

The strain compounds with Paper 2's demonstration that the QCD vacuum structure is a geometric feature of extra-dimensional space. The framework cannot express either the virtual infrastructure (Paper 1) or the geometric origin of the vacuum (Paper 2), and these are not peripheral curiosities — they concern the mass generation mechanism and vacuum structure of the strong force, i.e., the origin of ~99% of visible mass.

**T082 — Non-uniqueness of permanently virtual infrastructure: the computation underlying reality is underdetermined even beyond the number field**
Opened: this cycle
Description: Calcagni & Briscese (2603.25709) prove that for fractional field theories with $0 < \gamma < 3$, different self-adjoint formulations of the kinetic term produce different distributions of complex-mass virtual modes but identical physical spectra and identical S-matrix unitarity. The framework's "reality is literally computation" faces a new underdetermination that is *worse* than T069 (number field ℝ vs. ℂ) in several ways: (a) it is a *continuous* family of underdeterminations, not a binary choice; (b) it concerns the computational infrastructure (virtual modes) that T076 showed is constitutively necessary for physical mass generation; (c) it cannot be dismissed as "different descriptions of the same computation" (T010) because the virtual mode spectra are genuinely different mathematical objects — different sets of complex numbers, different residues, different distributions in the complex mass plane — that merely produce the same on-shell physics. The framework must either (a) declare that only the on-shell/resolvable content is "reality" and the virtual infrastructure is ontologically null — but T076 showed this infrastructure generates confinement, making it physically indispensable; (b) declare all formulations equally "real," making reality a multiverse of virtual structures underlying a single set of observations — which is precisely the unfalsifiable metaphysics the framework was designed to avoid; (c) find a physical criterion selecting one virtual mode distribution over others — but the paper proves they produce identical physical spectra, so no physical criterion can exist; or (d) abandon the "reality is literally computation" claim in favor of "reality is literally the equivalence class of computations producing the same resolvable physics" — but this is a substantive weakening, since equivalence classes are abstract mathematical objects, not computations.
Why unresolved: The fakeon prescription, which determines which modes are physical and which are projected out, is the mechanism enforcing the boundary between resolved and permanently virtual. This prescription is a choice made at the level of theory definition, not a consequence of any dynamical process. The framework has no primitive for theory-level choices (T057). The non-uniqueness is exact and proven, not approximate or conjectural, and it applies to gravitational theories ($\gamma > 2$ gives super-renormalizable quantum gravity), meaning it directly concerns the framework's primary example (gravity). The framework is now underdetermined at *three* nested levels: the number field (T069), the propagator power (which γ), and the virtual mode distribution (which self-adjoint formulation), with none of these determinable from within.
