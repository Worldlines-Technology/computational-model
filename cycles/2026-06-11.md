### PAPERS PROCESSED

**Paper 1: "Fermions are fundamentally more nonlocal than Bosons"**
ArXiv ID: 2606.12363v1 | Selection type: Strain

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper demonstrates that the particle statistics type — fermionic vs. bosonic — constitutes an irreducible operational resource that determines the nonlocal computational capacity of a quantum network. The framework treats fields as having gradients that things roll down, and treats conservation as a universal, undifferentiated principle. The paper proves that fermionic anticommutation relations generate correlations in quantum networks that are *strictly stronger* than anything achievable with bosonic or distinguishable-particle carriers, even when augmented with classical communication. This is not a quantitative enhancement but a provable separation: there exist distributed computing tasks where fermions succeed deterministically and all qubit/boson protocols fail without additional nonlocal resources. The framework has no primitive, parameter, or mechanism that distinguishes fermionic from bosonic computational capacity. The Conservation primitive treats all "computation" as fungible; the Resolution primitive treats all measurement outcomes as equivalent regardless of the carrier's statistics. The paper proves they are not fungible: fermionic information (febits) is a categorically distinct resource from bosonic information (qubits), with the distinction traceable to the antisymmetry of the wavefunction under particle exchange — an algebraic/topological property of the state space that no primitive can express. The framework's existing note on field-specific singularity survival (T040/T044) established that fermion and boson fields have different resilience properties; this paper extends the fermion/boson asymmetry from passive resilience to *active computational superiority*. The framework's implicit assumption that "computation is computation" — that the computational content is independent of the carrier type — is falsified by the communication complexity separation.

**Paper 2: "Nonadditivity in Quantum Field Theory: Replica Energies, Scaling Filters, and the Renormalization Group"**
ArXiv ID: 2606.12367v1 | Selection type: Strain

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper demonstrates that the physically meaningful content of quantum field theories — central charges, anomaly coefficients, universal entanglement data, RG irreversibility — is extracted not by any of the five primitives but by a differential filtering operation $(1 - \frac{1}{d}L\partial_L)$ that isolates the *nonadditive* part of the partition function. The framework's Conservation primitive treats computational content as a single conserved quantity; the paper shows that the physically interesting content is precisely the part that violates extensive (additive) scaling. The replica energy $\mathcal{E}$ measures the failure of the partition function to scale homogeneously — i.e., the failure of computational content to be simply additive across subsystems. The framework has no concept of additivity vs. nonadditivity of computational content, no mechanism for the scaling filter that extracts universal data, and no account of why the *nonadditive* part contains the universal physics (central charges, F-theorem, a-theorem) while the additive part is non-universal (bulk free energy, local counterterms). The paper's unified treatment of finite-size corrections, topological constants in gapped phases, fracton degeneracies, and self-gravitating thermal matter under a single nonadditivity framework demonstrates that the organizational principle cutting across these phenomena is *failure of extensivity* — a thermodynamic/information-theoretic concept that the framework's five dynamical primitives cannot express. The connection to RG irreversibility (the a-theorem, F-theorem) is especially challenging: the paper shows that the monotonically decreasing quantities under RG flow are specific nonadditivity measures, meaning the arrow of the renormalization group — arguably the most fundamental organizational principle in QFT — is a statement about the monotonic decrease of a specific nonadditive quantity. The framework's silence on the renormalization group (T080, T108) is deepened: not only can the framework not express scale evolution, but the quantity whose decrease *defines* the direction of scale evolution is the replica energy — a nonadditivity measure the framework has no vocabulary for. The distinction between fracton-type subextensive degeneracy ($\mathcal{E}$ scales as a fractional power of volume) and genuinely nonextensive systems (self-gravitating matter where $\mathcal{E}$ scales superextensively) adds a new classification axis for the Attractor primitive's constrained-attractor category that the primitive cannot express.

**Paper 3: "A post-selected quantum model of cosmic acceleration"**
ArXiv ID: 2606.12297v1 | Selection type: Validation

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper presents a model where cosmic acceleration arises from quantum post-selection — a future boundary condition on the cosmological wavefunction — rather than from a cosmological constant, dark energy, or modified gravity. The framework's dark energy claim ("the computational cost of instantiating new substrate") is *not validated* by this paper; rather, the paper adds yet another alternative mechanism to the already long list (T007 ensemble averaging, T086 unimodular gravity, dark matter particle creation, baby universe absorption, unified scalar field, quantum post-selection from 2026-06-02). The framework's Resolution primitive describes the arrow of time as the accumulation of resolved states, implying a forward-only causal structure. The post-selection model requires a future boundary state to shape intermediate dynamics — a final-condition effect that the Resolution primitive cannot represent and that was already flagged (in the 2026-06-02 model update and T159's related tension about causal-prescription infrastructure). The statistical competitiveness of the model (comparable to ΛCDM with fewer or equal parameters) and its natural resolution of the coincidence problem demonstrate that the observational data the framework's dark energy claim was designed to explain are equally well explained by a mechanism (teleological quantum boundary conditions) that contradicts the framework's forward-only causal picture. The prediction of a jerk parameter different from ΛCDM provides a potential discriminating test, but this prediction comes from the post-selection model's internal structure, not from the Worldlines framework. The framework has zero predictive purchase here: it cannot predict the existence, magnitude, or parametric structure of post-selection-induced acceleration. This is post-hoc consistency at its weakest — the framework's vocabulary can narrate the result ("the computational process has a boundary condition") but contributed no prediction, constraint, or distinguishing feature. The paper's demonstration that standard early-time cosmology (radiation- and matter-dominated eras) is preserved while late-time acceleration is generated by post-selection means the framework's Tick and Field primitives are unchallenged for early-time dynamics but irrelevant for the late-time phenomenon the framework specifically claims to explain.

### FRAMEWORK STRAINS

The most severe strain this cycle comes from Paper 1's proof that fermionic and bosonic computational carriers are *provably inequivalent* in their nonlocal computational capacity, with fermions strictly surpassing all qubit/boson protocols for specific distributed tasks. This strikes at the framework's core claim more precisely than most previous tensions. The framework asserts "reality is literally computation" and treats computation as a single, undifferentiated category whose content is conserved. Conservation of Computation implicitly assumes computational content is fungible — it "cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed." Paper 1 proves that fermionic computational content *cannot be transformed into* equivalent bosonic computational content, even with unlimited classical communication. The febit (fermionic information carrier) is a strictly more powerful resource than the qubit for specific tasks, and this superiority traces to the antisymmetry of the fermionic wavefunction — a property of the state space's exchange topology, not of any dynamical process.

The framework has no primitive for particle statistics. It has no parameter distinguishing fermions from bosons. It has no mechanism for the anticommutation relations that generate the nonlocal advantage. The Field primitive's gradient equation treats all fields identically; the Conservation primitive treats all computational content as equivalent; the Resolution primitive treats all measurement outcomes as equivalent regardless of carrier type. The paper proves all three of these equivalences are physically wrong: the carrier's statistics type determines which correlations are achievable, which tasks are solvable, and what computational resources are available.

This is not merely another instance of the structural content gap (T026) — it is a proof that the framework's most basic ontological category ("computation") has an internal structure (statistics type) that is operationally consequential and provably non-fungible. The framework cannot be patched by adding a parameter; the entire Conservation primitive's transformation clause must be qualified to acknowledge that certain types of computational content are categorically non-interconvertible.

Paper 2's strain is also significant but operates at a different level. The replica energy framework demonstrates that the physically universal content of QFT is specifically the *nonadditive* part of the partition function — the part that violates extensive scaling. The framework has no concept of extensivity or its failure, no mechanism for the differential filter extracting universal data, and no account of why universality is encoded in nonadditivity. The direction of RG flow being defined by monotonic decrease of a nonadditivity measure means the most fundamental organizational arrow in QFT (UV → IR) is a statement about nonadditivity, and the framework cannot express either the quantity or its monotonicity.
