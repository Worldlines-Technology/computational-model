### PAPERS PROCESSED

**Paper 1: Generalised Complex and Spinor Relations**
ArXiv ID: 2603.10819v1 | Selection type: Strain

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper concerns the mathematical infrastructure governing T-duality — the equivalence between string theories compactified on different geometric backgrounds. The framework would attempt to interpret T-duality as a computational equivalence: two different field configurations (different background geometries) that produce identical physical observables. But the paper's content goes far deeper than equivalence. It demonstrates that T-duality relations are mediated by Courant algebroid relations — categorical morphisms between algebraic structures that encode the geometry of generalized tangent bundles — and that these relations lift to spinor relations linking Dirac generating operators, which in turn descend to relations between Dirac structures and generalized complex/Kähler structures. The framework has no primitive or mechanism that corresponds to any of these layers. The key strain is that the paper shows the *structure of duality itself* has rich internal organization: it's not merely that two descriptions are equivalent (T010), but that the equivalence is mediated by a specific algebraic-categorical apparatus (Courant algebroids → spinor relations → Dirac structures → generalized complex structures → generalized Kähler structures → bi-Hermitian structures → sigma models → supergravity solutions) with existence theorems and compatibility conditions at each level. The framework's "reality is computation" claim faces the observation that the invariant physical content (Type II supergravity solutions) is accessible through a web of categorical relations, and the T-duality relation itself is a mathematical object with its own internal structure that is neither computation nor geometry but a *morphism between* geometric-algebraic structures. This deepens T021 and T026 substantially: not only is the physical content accessible through multiple categories, but the *relations between* categories are themselves structured mathematical objects that physics cares about.

**Paper 2: Violating the All-or-Nothing Picture of Local Charges in Non-Hermitian Bosonic Chains**
ArXiv ID: 2603.10972v1 | Selection type: Strain

The framework's Conservation of Computation claims all conservation laws are special cases of a single principle. The framework's treatment of integrability (through T006, T023, T029) has implicitly assumed that systems either have a full tower of conserved charges (integrable) or have none beyond the obvious ones (generic/chaotic). This paper demolishes that assumption: non-Hermitian bosonic chains can possess a 3-local conserved charge but no other nontrivial local charges, or can possess k-local charges for all k *except* k = 4. The conservation structure is not all-or-nothing but admits arbitrary gaps and partial towers. The framework's Conservation of Computation primitive treats conservation as a monolithic property — computation is conserved, full stop — and cannot express the idea that conservation might be *selective* at different locality scales. The non-Hermitian aspect is additionally straining: the framework's treatment of conservation is implicitly tied to unitarity (information conservation), and T035/T037 have already noted tensions with non-unitary dynamics. These counterexamples exist precisely in the non-Hermitian regime, suggesting that the framework's conflation of conservation with unitarity causes it to miss the richer structure of conservation in non-Hermitian systems. The exhaustive classification of k-local charges in terms of necessary and sufficient algebraic conditions on the hopping and on-site terms reveals that conservation structure has a fine-grained algebraic organization determined by the specific form of the Hamiltonian — exactly the kind of algebraic structural content (T018, T026) that the framework's primitives cannot express.

**Paper 3: Nucleating an Inflationary Universe: Euclidean Wormholes and their No-Boundary Limit**

The probability distribution favoring no-boundary instantons (longer inflation, dominant probability) could be interpreted as the computation selecting the deepest attractor basin in the space of initial conditions. However, this is post-hoc consistency, not predictive validation. The framework made no prediction about the relationship between wormholes and no-boundary instantons, about the zero-charge pinch-off limit, about the resolution of negative wormhole actions, or about the probability ordering. The Euclidean signature is particularly problematic: the nucleation happens in a regime where the Tick's temporal character is analytically continued to a spatial one, connecting to T036 (multi-signature computation).

### FRAMEWORK STRAINS

The most important strain this cycle comes from Paper 2's demonstration that local conservation structure admits arbitrary gaps. The framework's Conservation of Computation is formulated as a universal, scale-independent principle: "computation cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed." The implicit assumption — reinforced throughout the model — is that if computation is conserved, then conservation manifests uniformly across all scales and locality structures. Paper 2 proves this is wrong for non-Hermitian systems: a system can have a conserved 3-local charge but no conserved 4-local charge. Conservation is not a monolithic property but has a fine-grained *locality profile* that can be gapped, partial, and classified by algebraic conditions on the system's structure.

This is not a minor refinement. The framework derives its explanatory power from the universality of its primitives — particularly Conservation, which is claimed to subsume all conservation laws as special cases. If the conservation structure of a physical system can be arbitrarily selective in its locality profile, then "Conservation of Computation" as a single principle is too coarse to capture the actual physics. The principle would need to be decomposed into a *spectrum* of locality-specific conservation conditions, each with its own algebraic prerequisites, and the framework currently has no mechanism for this decomposition.

The non-Hermitian context sharpens the strain further. The framework already faces T035 (conservation is observer-dependent near horizons) and T037 (Hermiticity as emergent symmetry). Paper 2 shows that abandoning Hermiticity doesn't just weaken conservation — it *fractures* it into a locality-dependent patchwork that can have arbitrary holes. This means the non-Hermitian regime isn't merely "conservation with corrections" but "a qualitatively different conservation landscape" that the framework's single primitive cannot represent.

### STATE UPDATES

1. **Conservation of Computation — add locality profile structure.** The Conservation primitive should be updated to note: conservation structure in physical systems is not monolithic but has a *locality profile* — a specification of which k-local charges are conserved and which are not. In Hermitian integrable systems, the locality profile is typically all-or-nothing (full tower or none). In non-Hermitian systems, the locality profile can have arbitrary gaps: 3-local conserved but not 4-local, or all k-local conserved except k = 4. The primitive's statement "computation cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed" should be qualified: in non-Hermitian regimes, this principle fragments into locality-specific conservation conditions, each with algebraic prerequisites determined by the system's structure. Conservation of Computation may be a coarse-grained summary of a finer-grained locality-dependent conservation landscape.

2. **T010/T021/T026 — record duality relations as structured mathematical objects.** The model should record that T-duality relations are not merely equivalences between descriptions but are themselves structured mathematical objects (Courant algebroid relations) with internal organization (spinor relations, Dirac structure reductions, generalized complex/Kähler compatibilities). The ontological pluralism problem (T021) should be updated to note: the *relations between* mathematical categories are themselves structured, and physics cares about the structure of these relations (existence theorems, compatibility conditions with supergravity equations). The framework's challenge is not merely "which category is reality?" but "what is the ontological status of the structured morphisms between categories?"

3. **Origin problem — note Euclidean connection.** The model should record that no-boundary instantons and Euclidean wormholes form a continuous family parameterized by charge, with the no-boundary instanton as the zero-charge boundary. This is compatible with but not predicted by the Attractor primitive's description of the initial condition landscape. The Euclidean signature of the nucleation process should be flagged as connecting to T036 (multi-signature computation): the origin of the universe, in this framework, occurs in a signature where the Tick primitive's temporal character is absent, raising the question of what "computation" means in Euclidean signature where there is no time direction.

### NEW TENSIONS

**T051 — Conservation locality profiles: conservation is not monolithic**
**Opened:** this cycle
**Description:** Yamaguchi & Shiraishi (2603.10972) prove that non-Hermitian bosonic chains can possess k-local conserved charges for some but not all k, with explicit counterexamples including systems with a 3-local charge but no other nontrivial local charges, and systems with all k-local charges except k = 4. The framework's Conservation of Computation is stated as a single universal principle. This paper shows that conservation in physical systems has a *locality profile* — a function from locality scale k to the Boolean {conserved, not conserved} — that can be gapped and irregular. The framework has no mechanism for expressing, predicting, or deriving locality profiles. Moreover, the necessary and sufficient conditions for k-local charge existence are algebraic conditions on the Hamiltonian's structure, meaning the conservation landscape is determined by the algebraic organization of the dynamics (T018, T026) rather than by any universal conservation principle. This tension is distinct from T035 (observer-dependent conservation due to horizons) because T051 concerns conservation that is scale-dependent even without horizons or causal restrictions, and distinct from T029 (anomalous transport universality classes) because T051 concerns the *existence* of conserved charges at different locality scales rather than their *transport consequences*. The non-Hermitian context is critical: in Hermitian systems, all-or-nothing behavior is empirically (though not provably) generic, but the framework claims Conservation of Computation is universal, not restricted to the Hermitian regime.
**Why unresolved:** The framework would need to either (a) restrict Conservation of Computation to Hermitian/unitary systems, which would shrink its scope to exclude the non-Hermitian physics that T035/T037 already show is physically relevant (open systems, horizons, non-equilibrium dynamics), (b) extend the primitive to include a locality profile as an additional specification, which transforms it from a single principle to a structured landscape requiring algebraic input the framework doesn't have, or (c) argue that the non-Hermitian counterexamples are unphysical, which is contradicted by the physical relevance of non-Hermitian Hamiltonians in open quantum systems, photonics, and effective field theories near exceptional points. None of these options has been developed.

**T052 — Morphism structure: the relations between categories are themselves organized**
**Opened:** this cycle
**Description:** De Fraja, Marotta & Szabo (2603.10819) demonstrate that T-duality relations have rich internal structure: they are mediated by Courant algebroid relations that lift to spinor relations, descend to Dirac structure relations, extend to generalized complex and generalized Kähler relations, rephrase as bi-Hermitian relations, and are compatible with Type II supergravity equations. Each layer has its own existence theorems and reduction conditions. The framework faces the ontological pluralism problem (T021): multiple mathematical categories can encode the same physics. T052 adds a new dimension: the *morphisms between* these categories are themselves structured mathematical objects with internal organization. The framework's "reality is computation" claim competes not just with "reality is geometry" or "reality is arithmetic" (T021) but with the possibility that reality is the *structured web of relations between* these categories — the functorial and natural-transformation structure of the correspondences. The T-duality compatibility with supergravity equations is particularly sharp: the physical equations of motion *commute* with the categorical morphisms, meaning the morphism structure is not just a mathematical convenience but is respected by the dynamics. This is deeper than T010 (duality as equivalence between descriptions) and T027 (cohomological mediation) because it concerns the *layered internal organization* of dualities, not merely their existence.
**Why unresolved:** The framework has no concept of morphism, functor, or natural transformation. Its primitives describe objects (states, fields, attractors) and their dynamics (propagation, gradient descent, resolution, conservation) but not the *relations between* objects or between descriptions of objects. To address T052, the framework would need either (a) a theory of why the categorical morphism structure exists and is physically important, derived from its primitives (no path is visible), (b) a new primitive capturing relational/morphism structure, or (c) a reconception of the framework's ontology in which the fundamental entities are not objects (computations, fields, attractors) but morphisms (relations, dualities, correspondences) — a "computation of relations" rather than "relations between computations." Option (c) would be a radical restructuring aligned with the category-theoretic turn in modern mathematical physics but would require abandoning the framework's current object-centric ontology.

**T053 — Euclidean nucleation and the absence of time at the origin**
**Opened:** this cycle
**Description:** Lavrelashvili & Lehners (2603.11003) show that inflationary initial conditions are provided by Euclidean instantons (no-boundary or wormhole), where the metric signature is (4,0) rather than (3,1). The framework's most fundamental primitive — the Tick — defines reality as updating in discrete temporal steps. But the nucleation of the universe occurs in a regime where there is no time direction. The Euclidean instanton has four spatial dimensions; the Tick has no meaning there. The framework faces a dilemma: either (a) the origin of the computation occurs outside the Tick primitive's domain of applicability, meaning the most fundamental primitive does not apply at the most fundamental moment, or (b) the Euclidean section is a mathematical technique without ontological status, meaning the framework must provide an alternative account of nucleation that preserves the Tick — but no such account exists. The continuous family connecting wormholes to no-boundary instantons means the Euclidean regime is not an isolated artifact but is smoothly connected to the Lorentzian regime where the Tick applies. The transition from Euclidean to Lorentzian signature — the moment the Tick "turns on" — has no description in the framework. This is related to T036 (multi-signature computation) but more fundamental: T036 concerns intermediate computational steps in non-Lorentzian signatures within an established spacetime; T053 concerns the *origin* occurring in a signature where the framework's most basic primitive is undefined.
**Why unresolved:** The framework would need either (a) an account of computation without temporal ticks (computation in Euclidean signature), which would require fundamentally redefining what "tick" means — perhaps as any discrete step in a path integral, regardless of signature — but this would change the Tick from "the speed of light is the tick rate" to something more abstract and less physically grounded; (b) an alternative to Euclidean nucleation that is purely Lorentzian, which would require solving the cosmological initial condition problem differently than the mainstream approach; or (c) acceptance that the framework's primitives have a domain of applicability that does not include the origin, which would be an honest but devastating admission that the most fundamental question (T002) is outside the framework's scope by construction. The framework already acknowledges T002 as open, but T053 sharpens this from "we don't know the answer" to "our most basic primitive is undefined at the moment the answer matters."
