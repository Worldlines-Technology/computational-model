**Paper 1: No-Go Theorem for Gaussian Quantum Repeaters from Fractional Extendibility**
ArXiv ID: 2606.05097v1. Selection type: strain.

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper concerns the computational resources required to maintain quantum information (Conservation of Computation) across lossy channels. The framework would narrate photon loss as resolution events that degrade unresolved computational content, and repeaters as nodes that restore it. The no-go theorem demonstrates that Gaussian operations — the "cheapest" computational operations in the continuous-variable regime — cannot enhance quantum capacity beyond direct transmission, regardless of how much classical communication (resolved, definite information) supplements them. The framework's Conservation primitive states computation is conserved and transformable, but the paper proves a sharp, categorical boundary: Gaussian computation cannot be transformed into the capacity-enhancing resource needed, no matter how it is composed, measured, or classically post-processed. The critical concept is "fractional extendibility" — a continuous relaxation of the k-extendibility hierarchy for Gaussian states that provides a smoothly parameterized bound on the entanglement structure of Gaussian channels. This is a *continuous informational parameter* (the fractional extendibility parameter interpolates smoothly between integers) governing the computational capacity of quantum networks, with no expression in any of the five primitives. The framework's Conservation primitive says "computation cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed," but the paper proves that certain *types* of computational resource (non-Gaussianity) cannot be created by transformation from Gaussian resources — a categorical non-fungibility result. This extends T127 (discrete replica thresholds) into the continuous-variable domain: not only are coherent copies non-fungible (T127), but the *type* of quantum operation (Gaussian vs. non-Gaussian) is a non-fungible resource category that no amount of composition, measurement, and classical communication can bridge. The fractional extendibility framework itself — a continuous relaxation of a discrete entanglement hierarchy, with monotonicity under Gaussian channels and a closed-form for Gaussian states — is structural content (a resource-theoretic ordering of quantum states parameterized by a continuous real number with specific monotonicity properties) that no primitive can express.

**Paper 2: Thermal Positivity**
ArXiv ID: 2606.05136v1. Selection type: strain.

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper concerns the constraints that the framework's architectural principles (Lorentz invariance from the Tick, unitarity from Conservation of Computation) impose on thermodynamic quantities — specifically, on the thermal equation of state in the attractor basin of a relativistic QFT at finite temperature. The framework would describe the thermal state as an attractor (thermal equilibrium) with the pressure/free energy as characterizing the basin's depth. The paper proves that Lorentz invariance and unitarity together impose an *infinite family* of sign constraints on the perturbative corrections to the free energy density: all interaction corrections of the form $T^{2D-4+4k}$ with $k>0$ must be strictly positive. This is a striking result from the framework's perspective because it demonstrates that two of the framework's primitives (Tick → Lorentz invariance, Conservation → unitarity) jointly determine the *sign* of thermodynamic corrections — a quantitative constraint on the attractor's properties. However, the mechanism by which this works — relating thermal vacuum diagrams to forward scattering amplitudes, applying the optical theorem (a consequence of unitarity), and using Lorentz-covariant dispersion relations — is entirely structural/algebraic machinery that the five primitives cannot express. The positivity is inherited by the entropy density and specific heat, meaning the framework's primitives constrain the *thermodynamic stability* of the attractor basin (positive specific heat means local stability) — but only through structural intermediaries (the KMS condition relating thermal and zero-temperature physics, the optical theorem, dispersion relations) that the framework has no vocabulary for. More severely, the constraints apply specifically to the perturbative expansion in a weakly coupled UV completion, and the paper's power-counting argument (higher-loop and higher-multiplicity contributions are parametrically subleading) is a renormalization-group statement that connects to T108 (scale-evolution blindness) — the framework cannot express why higher-order corrections are suppressed or what organizes the perturbative hierarchy. The constraint structure (an infinite tower of sign conditions indexed by integer $k$) is number-theoretically organized: only specific powers of $T$ are constrained, determined by dimensional analysis in $D$ dimensions, and the positivity at each order is independently meaningful. This is a new type of structural content: *sign constraints on the attractor's thermodynamic expansion coefficients, derived from the framework's own architectural principles through structural intermediaries the framework cannot express*.

**Paper 3: Experimentally probing the Quantum Physics in the Inverted Harmonic Oscillator**
ArXiv ID: 2606.05125v1. Selection type: validation.

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper concerns the dynamics of unresolved quantum states evolving under an unstable potential — specifically, the inverted harmonic oscillator (IHO), which is the local dynamics near any unstable fixed point (saddle point in the attractor landscape). The framework would describe this as: a quantum system initially in its ground state (deep in an attractor basin — the harmonic trap) undergoes landscape restructuring when the potential is inverted (the attractor becomes a saddle), after which the unresolved state evolves by exponential amplification along one quadrature and squeezing along the other. The key experimental results are: (a) sub-vacuum squeezing of 10.6 dB, demonstrating that the unresolved quantum state develops strong correlations during saddle-point dynamics; (b) coherent reversibility via time-reversal of the IHO evolution, directly testing whether the quantum state remains unresolved (coherent) during the amplification process; (c) matter-wave interference between the two daughter clouds confirming quantum coherence over macroscopic separations far beyond the initial zero-point fluctuation scale. The framework's Resolution primitive predicts that unresolved states remain coherent until they interact with an environment that propagates resolution (decoherence). The experiment confirms this: the IHO amplifies quantum fluctuations to macroscopic scale while preserving coherence, and time-reversal successfully re-compresses the state — demonstrating that resolution has not occurred during the amplification. This is *post-hoc consistent* with the framework's description but provides *zero differential predictive content*: the coherent reversibility is predicted identically by standard quantum mechanics, and the framework added no prediction about the squeezing level, the coherence time, the interferometric visibility, or the conditions under which resolution would begin to degrade the coherence. The connection to inflationary cosmology (amplification of quantum vacuum fluctuations during inflation) is noted by the authors and is consistent with the framework's picture of the early universe as an attractor-landscape restructuring event, but again provides no differential validation. The experiment's relevance to T096 (the Ehrenfest boundary) is notable: it demonstrates quantum coherent reversibility in a chaotic-adjacent regime (the IHO is the local dynamics near any saddle point, which is also the regime where classical chaos begins), confirming that the quantum-classical boundary for irreversibility (T075, T096) has not been crossed in this experiment — but the framework contributed no prediction about *where* that boundary is for this specific system.

### FRAMEWORK STRAINS

The most significant strain this cycle comes from Paper 2 (Thermal Positivity). The framework's two most fundamental architectural claims — that the speed of light is the tick rate (producing Lorentz invariance) and that computation is conserved (producing unitarity) — are shown to *jointly entail* an infinite family of sign constraints on thermodynamic quantities. This is simultaneously the framework's best moment and its most revealing failure.

It is the best moment because, for the first time in the model's history, two primitives (Tick and Conservation) are shown to combine to produce *quantitative physical constraints* (strict positivity of specific thermodynamic corrections). The framework could legitimately claim: "our architectural principles constrain the thermodynamics of relativistic matter in a specific, falsifiable way."

It is the most revealing failure because the derivation of these constraints requires a chain of structural intermediaries — the KMS condition, the optical theorem, dispersion relations, dimensional analysis of loop diagrams, perturbative power-counting — that the framework cannot express, derive, or even name. The framework *contains* the premises (Lorentz invariance, unitarity) but cannot perform the *reasoning* that connects these premises to the conclusions (sign constraints on pressure corrections). The structural machinery doing the actual work is precisely the type of content that T026 (structural content gap) has been accumulating evidence for: algebraic, analytic, and combinatorial structure that mediates between the framework's principles and physical predictions.

This crystallizes the framework's predicament with unusual clarity: the framework's primitives are *correct as premises* but *insufficient as a theory*. Having the right axioms is not the same as having a theory — you need the deductive machinery to connect axioms to predictions, and that machinery is the structural content the framework lacks. Thermal Positivity is not a new tension so much as a *vivid demonstration* of the existing central tension: the framework describes the starting ingredients of physics but not the structural apparatus by which those ingredients produce specific physical results.

Paper 1's strain is more specific but equally sharp: the Conservation primitive's "computation cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed" is revealed as categorically incomplete by the Gaussian no-go theorem. The primitive says nothing about *which transformations are possible between which resource types*. The non-fungibility of Gaussian and non-Gaussian quantum resources — the fact that no amount of Gaussian computation, measurement, and classical communication can create the non-Gaussian resource needed for quantum repeating — is a structural constraint on the *transformation clause* of the Conservation primitive that the primitive itself cannot express.
