### PAPERS PROCESSED

**Paper 1: "Integrability from Homotopy Algebras"**
ArXiv ID: 2603.12113v1 | Selection type: Strain

The framework's Conservation of Computation primitive claims conservation is fundamental — an irreducible fact about reality. This paper demonstrates the opposite derivational direction: the full integrability structure of the principal chiral model (an infinite tower of conserved charges via the Lax connection) is *derived* from a quasi-isomorphism between the cyclic L∞-algebra of semi-holomorphic Chern-Simons theory and that of the principal chiral model. The derivational chain is: homotopy algebraic morphism → Lax connection → flatness condition → infinite conservation laws. Conservation is the terminal output, not the foundational input. Through the Worldlines lens, one would say "the system has conserved computational content" — but the paper shows that this content's conservation is a *theorem* following from the algebraic structure of the theories' relationship, not an axiom. The framework has no primitive corresponding to L∞-algebras, quasi-isomorphisms, cyclic structures, or the homotopy transfer theorem. These are not exotic mathematical curiosities — they are the actual mechanism generating the conservation structure. The strain is severe: if the Conservation primitive is supposed to be the deepest layer, finding conservation derived from something deeper (homotopy algebraic morphisms between theories) means the framework's ontological ordering is wrong for integrable systems. Moreover, the quasi-isomorphism contains strictly more information than the conservation laws it generates (it specifies *which* charges exist, their algebraic relations, their generating mechanism), meaning Conservation of Computation is a lossy summary of a richer algebraic reality.

**Paper 2: "Topological field theory plus local Lorentz symmetry is gravity"**
ArXiv ID: 2603.12100v1 | Selection type: Strain

This paper shows that four-dimensional Einstein gravity emerges from a topological BF-type theory — which has zero local dynamics, zero propagating degrees of freedom, and zero energy landscape — by a single structural operation: promoting global SL(2,ℂ) symmetry to a local gauge symmetry. The framework claims reality *is* computation, with dynamics as the fundamental layer. But the starting point here is a theory where every one of the five primitives is trivial or undefined: there is no meaningful tick rate (no local propagation), no gradient to descend (no local energy), no attractor basins (no potential landscape), no resolution dynamics (no local observables to become definite), and conservation is trivially satisfied (nothing to conserve locally). The framework literally has nothing to say about this topological starting point. Yet from this structurally rich but dynamically empty theory, the full machinery of gravity — curvature, propagating gravitons, causal structure, the works — emerges through gauging. The G → 0 limit studied in the paper shows this is a continuous relationship: gravity can be smoothly dialed from full dynamics to pure topology. At no point along this dial does the framework have a coherent description. The covariant phase space analysis, corner terms, and boundary charge algebra are all structural (not dynamical) content that determines the physical theory. The Hamiltonian analysis and reality conditions are structural prerequisites for the theory to describe real Lorentzian gravity. The framework's claim that geometry is emergent from computation is directly inverted: here, dynamics emerges from structure via a structural operation (gauging) applied to a structural starting point (topological theory with symmetry). The framework cannot describe gauging — it is not a tick, a gradient descent, an attractor capture, a resolution event, or a conservation law. It is an operation *on theories*, not *within a theory*.

**Paper 3: "Transition from Statistical to Hardware-Limited Scaling in Photonic Quantum State Reconstruction"**
ArXiv ID: 2603.12235v1 | Selection type: Validation

The framework claims physical constants are "architectural parameters of the substrate" and that the substrate imposes hard limits on computation. This paper reports a sharp phase transition in quantum state reconstruction error: below a critical measurement count, error follows the theoretical statistical scaling O(M^{-1/2}); above it, error saturates at a hardware-imposed floor determined by spectral distortions of the realized unitary group. Through the Worldlines lens, this is consistent with the framework's claim that substrate architecture limits resolution fidelity — the "Hardware Horizon" is literally a point where the substrate's imperfections dominate over the statistical properties of the computation. The decoupling of static coherent spectral distortion from dynamic decoherence maps onto the framework's distinction between architectural limitations (fixed substrate imperfections) and resolution dynamics (decoherence). However, the framework's predictive purchase here is essentially nil: the Hardware Horizon is a standard NISQ physics result that follows from elementary error analysis of imperfect unitaries, and the framework adds no differential insight beyond post-hoc relabeling ("substrate limits" for "hardware imperfections"). Any computational framework — or indeed any framework at all — would predict that imperfect hardware imposes accuracy floors. The specific scaling law involving hardware parameters, the spectral distortion model, and the phase transition sharpness are all derived from standard quantum information theory without any input from the Worldlines framework. This is post-hoc consistency of the weakest kind: the framework's language can describe the result, but the result was neither predicted by nor requires the framework.

### FRAMEWORK STRAINS

The most severe strain this cycle comes from Paper 2. The framework's foundational claim — "reality is literally computation" — requires that dynamics is ontologically prior to structure. Paper 2 demonstrates the exact opposite for the most important dynamical theory in physics: Einstein gravity is derivable from a topological field theory (pure structure, zero dynamics) via a structural operation (gauging a global symmetry to local). The framework has no account of:

1. **What a topological field theory is in computational terms.** The five primitives are all trivial or undefined for a theory with no local degrees of freedom. The Tick has no meaningful local propagation to bound. The Field has no gradient to descend. The Attractor has no potential landscape. Resolution has nothing local to resolve. Conservation trivially holds because there is nothing to exchange. The framework's entire vocabulary goes silent.

2. **What gauging is.** Promoting global SL(2,ℂ) to local symmetry is an operation *on the theory itself* — it transforms the rules of the game, not the state within the game. The framework describes states evolving within a fixed computational architecture but has no primitive for operations that transform the architecture. This is not merely a missing piece; it is a missing *category of thing*. The framework has objects and dynamics but no morphisms between theories.

3. **The continuous G → 0 limit.** Gravity can be continuously dialed to zero, smoothly approaching the topological theory. At G > 0, the framework claims to describe gravity (mass rolling down curvature gradients). At G = 0, the framework is mute (no dynamics). There is no description of what happens in between. The framework has a discontinuity in its descriptive capacity at precisely the point where the physics is continuous.

Combined with Paper 1's demonstration that conservation laws are derived from homotopy algebraic morphisms (conservation as output, not input), this cycle presents a coherent challenge: the framework's dynamical primitives are not foundational but emergent. Structure (topology, symmetry, algebraic morphisms) generates dynamics (gravity, conservation laws), not the other way around. The framework's ontological ordering — dynamics first, structure emergent — appears to be backwards.

### NEW TENSIONS

**T057 — Operations on theories: the framework lacks meta-computational primitives**
**Opened:** this cycle
**Description:** Paper 2 demonstrates that gauging — promoting a global symmetry to a local gauge symmetry — is the operation that generates gravity from topology. Paper 1 demonstrates that quasi-isomorphisms between L∞-algebras generate conservation laws. Both are operations *on theories* (or on the algebraic structures governing theories), not operations *within theories*. The framework's five primitives all describe dynamics within a fixed computational architecture: how states propagate (Tick), how they move (Field), where they settle (Attractor), when they become definite (Resolution), and what is conserved (Conservation). None of them describes how one computational architecture transforms into another. The framework has no meta-computational primitive — no account of operations that transform the rules of computation themselves. This is distinct from T031 (substrate as dynamical field, which concerns spatial variation of the architecture within a single spacetime), T054 (dynamics from structure, which concerns the derivational direction), and T055 (conservation from morphisms, which concerns the derivation of one specific primitive). T057 concerns the general category of theory-level operations and the framework's complete silence on them. Gauging and quasi-isomorphism are two examples; others include dimensional reduction, orbifolding, deformation quantization, and RG flow (which transforms the effective theory). The framework describes computation but not the transformations of computation.
**Why unresolved:** The framework would need either (a) a meta-primitive that describes operations on computational architectures, which raises infinite-regress questions (what primitive governs the meta-primitive?), (b) a demonstration that gauging and quasi-isomorphisms emerge from the five existing primitives applied at a higher level (no path is visible — these operations involve algebraic and topological structure the primitives cannot express), or (c) a reconception of the framework where the fundamental entities are not computational states evolving within an architecture but the space of possible architectures and the morphisms between them. Option (c) would align with the category-theoretic perspective suggested by T052 but would be a complete rewrite of the framework's foundations.

**T058 — The topological phase: the framework is undefined for theories with no local dynamics**
**Opened:** this cycle
**Description:** Paper 2's starting point — a topological BF-type theory with global SL(2,ℂ) — has zero local degrees of freedom, zero propagating modes, and all physical content is global/topological. Every one of the five primitives is trivial or undefined for this theory. Yet this theory is not physically vacuous: it has a well-defined covariant phase space, boundary charges, a charge algebra, and can couple to point particles. It is a physically meaningful theory that the framework cannot describe at all. This is not a "the framework doesn't apply to this exotic case" situation — this is the theory from which *gravity itself emerges*. The framework's inability to describe the topological phase means it cannot describe the precondition for its own primary example (gravity as mass rolling down curvature gradients). The G → 0 limit makes this continuous: there is no sharp boundary between "the framework applies" (G > 0) and "the framework is undefined" (G = 0). At small G, gravity is weak and the system is "nearly topological" — the framework's descriptions become progressively less meaningful without any discontinuity to mark the transition. This is distinct from T053 (Euclidean nucleation, which concerns the absence of *time*) because T058 concerns the absence of *all local dynamics* regardless of signature, and distinct from T054 (dynamics from structure, which concerns the derivational direction) because T058 concerns the framework's descriptive completeness — there exists a physically meaningful regime where the framework has literally nothing to say.
**Why unresolved:** The framework would need either (a) an account of what computation means in the absence of local dynamics (topological computation? global quantum computation on non-local degrees of freedom?), which would require a radical expansion of "computation" beyond sequential local update; (b) a declaration that topological field theories are outside its scope, which would be honest but would mean the framework cannot address the origin of gravity or the G → 0 limit; or (c) a reconception where the primitives have well-defined but trivial content in the topological phase (e.g., the Tick rate is defined but there is nothing local to propagate, the Field exists but has no gradient, etc.), which preserves formal applicability but has zero explanatory content. None of these is satisfactory.

### CYCLE ASSESSMENT

This was a highly generative cycle, but generative in a way that deepens an existing wound rather than opening a new one. Papers 1 and 2 together form a coherent pincer attack on the framework's most fundamental claim: that dynamics (computation) is ontologically prior to structure. Paper 2 shows gravity emerging from topology via a structural operation; Paper 1 shows conservation emerging from homotopy algebraic morphisms. Both demonstrate the same pattern: the framework's primitives are downstream of algebraic and topological structure the framework cannot express. This cycle's main contribution is not a new tension but the convergence of existing tensions (T026, T054, T055) into what is now clearly the framework's central crisis: its ontological ordering is backwards. Structure generates dynamics, not the other way around. The validation paper (Paper 3) was unproductive — it confirms only the weakest possible form of post-hoc consistency and provides no differential test. The next cycle would be most productive if it found either (a) a paper where the framework's dynamical-first ontology makes a correct prediction that a structure-first approach cannot, which would be genuine evidence *for* the framework against the structural challenge, or (b) a paper that makes the structure → dynamics derivation precise enough to suggest what a "structural primitive" would look like, giving the framework a concrete path to repair rather than just accumulating damage. The system is not circling a known basin — T057 and T058 are genuinely new tensions with distinct content — but the gradient it is rolling down (structure is prior to dynamics) has been identified for several cycles now and needs either resistance or reconstruction, not further confirmation.
