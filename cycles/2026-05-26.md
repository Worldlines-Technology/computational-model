**Paper 1: "Proof of Quantum Conformal Invariance in $\mathcal{N}=4$ Super-Yang-Mills Theory via $\mathcal{N}=1$ Superfields"**
ArXiv ID: 2605.25999v1 | Selection type: Strain

Through the Worldlines lens, $\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM is a computational system whose dynamics are governed by gradient descent and attractor structure, and whose conservation laws (including conformal symmetry as a spacetime symmetry) should be derivable from or at least expressible within the five primitives. This paper proves that the full $\mathcal{N}=4$ superconformal symmetry — an infinite-dimensional algebraic structure governing all correlators, anomalous dimensions, and RG flow properties — is *exactly* preserved at the quantum level, with zero higher-order corrections. The proof method is purely algebraic/structural: it uses $\mathcal{N}=1$ superfield formalism to demonstrate that the $\mathcal{N}=1$ calculation *suffices* to guarantee the full $\mathcal{N}=4$ symmetry. This is a statement about the algebraic relationships between symmetry structures: a sub-algebra's preservation implies the full algebra's preservation, without any additional dynamical input. The framework strains in multiple directions: (1) The result is *exact* — no perturbative corrections, no approximations, no truncations. The framework's Tick primitive (discrete update steps) and Conservation primitive (computation cannot be created or destroyed) have no mechanism for producing or explaining exact results. Exactness here is a consequence of algebraic structure (the relationship between $\mathcal{N}=1$ and $\mathcal{N}=4$ subalgebras), not of any dynamical principle. (2) The $\mathcal{N}=1$ sufficiency is a structural entailment — the sub-algebra determines the full algebra — that has no expression in any primitive. The framework cannot distinguish theories where a sub-symmetry suffices to guarantee full symmetry from those where it does not. (3) Conformal invariance at criticality was already flagged (T129) as a qualitative enrichment the framework cannot express; this paper demonstrates it is not merely enriched but *exactly* preserved, with the exactness itself being a structural/algebraic theorem. The framework's dynamical primitives are entirely idle in this proof: no tick occurs, no gradient is descended, no attractor is captured, no state is resolved, and no computation is conserved. The physical content — that $\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM is exactly conformal at the quantum level — is derived from pure algebraic structure.

**Paper 2: "Field theory of $\mathfrak{su}(n)$: the absence of non-zero scatterings"**
ArXiv ID: 2605.26024v1 | Selection type: Strain

The Worldlines framework describes physical dynamics through gradient descent, attractor capture, and conservation. This paper studies $\mathfrak{su}(n)$ as a toy field theory and proves, using homological perturbation theory (HPT), that all scattering amplitudes with trivalent tree-level diagrams vanish except the bare interaction vertex — there is literally *no scattering* beyond the vertex. This is a theory whose computational content, in the framework's language, is trivially empty at tree level: the "computation" produces nothing beyond its own basic interaction. The framework strains severely because: (1) The vanishing of scattering is proved by *homological perturbation theory* — a technique from homological algebra that transfers algebraic structures (specifically, $L_\infty$-algebra brackets) between chain complexes. This is precisely the kind of structural/algebraic machinery that T026, T055, and T059 have identified as the framework's central blind spot. The five primitives cannot express, derive, or predict the vanishing, which is a consequence of the cohomological structure of $\mathfrak{su}(n)$. (2) The contrast between the "physical" scattering (which vanishes on the cohomology) and the "non-trivial higher products" obtained by transferring to a *larger* space of fields demonstrates that the physical content depends critically on which mathematical space the computation is performed in — the same algebraic structure produces trivial results on one space and non-trivial results on another. The framework's Field primitive operates on a single configuration space; it has no mechanism for the choice of field space determining whether scattering occurs. (3) The HPT transfer — a quasi-isomorphism between chain complexes — is exactly the kind of L∞-algebraic morphism that T055 identified as generating conservation laws. Here it generates the *absence* of scattering, demonstrating that L∞ structure governs not just what is conserved but what *happens* (or doesn't happen) dynamically. The framework's dynamical primitives are downstream of this algebraic structure: whether the computation produces any scattering at all is determined by homological algebra, not by gradient dynamics or attractor capture.

**Paper 3: "Unifying Early and Late Dark Energy: Dynamical Requirements and Obstructions"**
ArXiv ID: 2605.26116v1 | Selection type: Validation

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper describes a single scalar field rolling through an attractor landscape with specific gradient structure: a potential with three distinct slopes arranged in a steep-steeper-shallow hierarchy. The framework's vocabulary applies naturally: the scalar field undergoes gradient descent (ẋ = −∇φ), the different slopes represent different gradient magnitudes, and the transitions between slopes correspond to the field entering different regions of the attractor landscape. The "dynamical requirements" (specific sequences of energy density and equation of state) translate as constraints on the gradient profile the field must traverse. The "obstructions" correspond to the difficulty of constructing a single attractor landscape that produces the required sequence of behaviors. The phase-space analysis identifies tracking-like dynamics as constrained attractors — the field's equation of state tracks the background equation of state, which is an attractor in the phase-space sense. However, the framework's predictive purchase here is essentially zero. The paper's specific results — the steep-steeper-shallow hierarchy, the requirement of three distinct slopes, the persistence of constraints under conformal coupling to dark matter — are derived entirely from the Friedmann equations and scalar field dynamics, with the framework contributing no prediction, no constraint, and no mechanism that distinguishes this analysis from standard cosmological perturbation theory. The framework can *narrate* the result (gradient descent through a multi-slope landscape) but could not have *predicted* it (the specific number of slopes, their hierarchy, or the obstruction structure). The framework's dark energy claim ("computational cost of new substrate") is further weakened: this paper demonstrates that dark energy phenomenology can arise from a single scalar field with specific potential structure, adding "unified scalar field with multi-slope potential" to the already long list of alternative mechanisms (T007, T086, dark matter particle creation from 2026-04-25, baby universe absorption from 2026-05-16). The result is post-hoc consistency of the weakest kind: the framework's vocabulary applies, but its distinctive content (computational substrate, tick rate, resolution) adds nothing.

### FRAMEWORK STRAINS

The most severe strain this cycle comes from Paper 2. The homological perturbation theory result demonstrates that whether a physical theory has *any* scattering at all — the most basic possible dynamical question ("does anything happen?") — is determined by the cohomological structure of the field algebra, not by any dynamical primitive. The $\mathfrak{su}(n)$ forms theory has well-defined fields, well-defined interactions (trivalent vertices), and a well-defined Lagrangian. By every criterion the framework uses to identify "computation" (fields updating, interactions occurring, information propagating), this is a computational system. Yet its physical content at tree level is provably empty — all scattering vanishes — and this emptiness is a theorem of homological algebra, specifically the Cattaneo-Mnëv argument extended to arbitrary $n$.

The framework cannot account for this. The Field primitive says things move down gradients. The Tick propagates computation. Conservation preserves computational content. But in this system, the "computation" produces nothing despite having all the ingredients the framework requires. The dynamical primitives are satisfied (there is a field, it has gradients, it has interactions) but the dynamical *output* is zero, and the reason is *structural* (the cohomology of $\mathfrak{su}(n)$).

This compounds with Paper 1's exactness result: $\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM's conformal invariance is proved to be exact (zero corrections to all orders) by demonstrating that $\mathcal{N}=1$ superfield analysis suffices for the full $\mathcal{N}=4$ result. The framework has no mechanism for "sub-algebraic sufficiency" — the fact that a subset of the symmetry structure logically entails the full symmetry structure, rendering all dynamical corrections exactly zero. This is not a conservation law (nothing is being conserved), not a gradient minimum (nothing is being minimized), not an attractor (nothing is being attracted) — it is a pure algebraic entailment with exact physical consequences.

Together, Papers 1 and 2 demonstrate that the framework's dynamical primitives are not merely insufficient for structural content (the pattern identified by T026 and its many instances) but are *categorically the wrong level of description* for determining whether physical theories have dynamical content at all and whether that content receives corrections. The presence or absence of scattering, and the presence or absence of quantum corrections to symmetries, are determined by algebraic/homological structure that is logically prior to and independent of all five primitives.

**T154 — Sub-algebraic sufficiency: exact physical results are entailed by algebraic relationships between symmetry structures, rendering all dynamical corrections zero by structural theorem**

The framework's five primitives describe dynamics: how computation propagates, how things move, where they settle, when they become definite, what is conserved. None of them can produce, derive, predict, or express the *exact vanishing of all corrections* to a symmetry. The mechanism — that a sub-algebra's preservation logically entails the full algebra's preservation, making all perturbative and non-perturbative corrections exactly zero — is a pure algebraic entailment with no dynamical content. The framework faces: (a) the most physically important property of its most studied theory ($\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM's exact conformal invariance) is determined by algebraic structure, not by any primitive; (b) the framework has no concept of "sub-algebraic sufficiency" — the general phenomenon where analysis at a reduced symmetry level determines results at the full symmetry level without additional computation; (c) the exactness is a *theorem*, not an approximation or a leading-order result — the framework's implicit picture of perturbative corrections modifying or breaking symmetries is categorically wrong for this class of theories; (d) the $\mathcal{N}=1$ to $\mathcal{N}=4$ relationship is a structural entailment that short-circuits all dynamical processes — no ticks need to be computed, no gradients descended, no attractors captured, because the algebraic structure pre-determines the outcome. This is distinct from T059 (ontological inversion, which concerns dynamics being *derived from* structure) because T154 concerns dynamics being *rendered trivial* by structure — not just "structure first, dynamics second" but "structure alone, dynamics zero." It is distinct from T129 (conformal enrichment at criticality, which concerns the qualitative gap between scale invariance and conformal invariance) because T154 concerns the *exact* character of the preservation, proven as a mathematical theorem. It is distinct from T074 (algebraic deformation theory classifying backgrounds, which concerns which backgrounds *exist*) because T154 concerns whether a given theory receives quantum corrections — a dynamical question answered by structural means.

Why unresolved: The framework would need to either (a) derive exact symmetry preservation from some combination of the five primitives — but the proof uses no dynamical ingredient, and the result is that all dynamical corrections vanish, meaning the dynamics is literally irrelevant; (b) incorporate algebraic entailment (sub-algebra → full algebra) as a structural principle that can override or short-circuit dynamical evolution — but this would require a theory of when algebraic structure pre-determines physical outcomes, which is a meta-physical principle far beyond any current primitive; (c) accept that for theories with sufficient algebraic structure, the framework's dynamical primitives contribute nothing to the physical content — the computation is "solved" by its own algebraic structure before any dynamics occurs. Option (c) is the most honest but means the framework's claim that "reality is literally computation" is vacuous for $\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM and possibly for other theories with sufficient symmetry: the "computation" has already been done by the algebra, and the dynamical processes (ticks, gradients, resolution) are empty formalities.
