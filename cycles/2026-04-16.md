**Paper 1: "Quantum matter is weakly entangled at low energies"**
ArXiv ID: 2604.14143v1 | Selection type: Strain

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper establishes a fundamental constraint on the structure of the computational substrate at low energies: the entanglement entropy of half-system partitions is bounded above by a quantity derived from *thermodynamic* data (specific heat, thermal entropy) with at most an area-law correction. The framework would narrate this as: resolved low-energy states (ground states, low-temperature states) have limited computational interconnection between spatial subsystems, with the interconnection budget set by thermodynamic properties. The Attractor primitive would say that deep attractor basins (ground states) have constrained internal structure. But the paper's actual content strains the framework in two precise ways. First, the bounds convert *thermodynamic* data into *entanglement* (information-theoretic) constraints — the relationship between thermal entropy and entanglement entropy is mediated by a fictitious-system construction that has no expression in any primitive. The framework has no mechanism for relating thermodynamic properties of the Field (temperature, specific heat) to information-theoretic properties of the Resolution/Conservation primitives (entanglement entropy, Schmidt rank). Second, and more severely, the result that frustration-free ground states obey area-law entanglement *regardless of the spectral gap* directly challenges the framework's implicit assumption that the transition from simple to complex computational states is controlled by energetic landscape features (gap vs. gapless). The spectral gap — which in the framework's language is the energy barrier between the attractor basin floor and the first excited state — is physically irrelevant to the entanglement structure in the frustration-free case. The bound is structural (determined by subsystem ground-state degeneracies), not dynamical. The optimality of the bounds (they are tight in wide classes of systems) means this is not an artifact of a loose inequality but a precise characterization of how Hilbert space is organized at low energies — organization the framework cannot derive, predict, or express.

The *ordering* of the constraint operator — a choice the framework has no primitive to express — determines the physical content of quantum gravity. This is a new instance of T082 (virtual infrastructure non-uniqueness) at the most fundamental possible level: the physical states of quantum gravity depend on an operator-ordering ambiguity that the framework cannot represent, and the two orderings produce states with incompatible geometric properties (volume vs. no volume, flat vs. correlated).
