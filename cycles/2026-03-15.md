### PAPERS PROCESSED

**Paper 1: Integrability from Homotopy Algebras** (arXiv: 2603.12113v1, selected for strain)

This paper constructs an explicit quasi-isomorphism between the cyclic L∞-algebras governing semi-holomorphic Chern-Simons theory (a 4D theory on a product manifold Σ₂ × ℂP¹) and the 2D principal chiral model, showing that the Lax connection — the mathematical object guaranteeing an infinite tower of conserved charges and hence integrability — is the direct image of this morphism. Through the Worldlines lens, the Conservation of Computation primitive would claim that the infinite conserved charges are expressions of the fundamental conservation law. But the paper inverts this: conservation is not the starting point but the *output* of a homotopy algebraic morphism between two distinct theories formulated in different dimensions. The quasi-isomorphism is constructive and explicit — it is a specific map between L∞-algebra structures (higher brackets L_n, cyclic inner products) that has no expression in any of the five primitives. The framework cannot describe what an L∞-algebra is, what a quasi-isomorphism between L∞-algebras does, or how a morphism in one algebraic category generates dynamical conservation in another. The derivational chain — cyclic L∞ quasi-isomorphism → Lax connection → flatness → infinite conserved charges → integrability — begins entirely outside the framework and terminates at the Conservation primitive, demonstrating that conservation is downstream of algebraic structure, not upstream of it. This paper is a direct second confirmation of the result already recorded as T055, now with full explicit construction rather than the earlier partial result. The dimensional reduction aspect (4D → 2D) adds a further strain: the conservation structure of a lower-dimensional theory is derived from a higher-dimensional topological/holomorphic theory, meaning the framework's flat treatment of dimensionality misses the generative role of extra dimensions in producing conservation laws.

**Paper 2: Topological field theory plus local Lorentz symmetry is gravity** (arXiv: 2603.12100v1, selected for strain)

This paper demonstrates that four-dimensional Einstein gravity (with or without cosmological constant) emerges from a topological BF-type theory with global SL(2,ℂ) symmetry when that symmetry is promoted to a local gauge symmetry. The starting theory has zero local degrees of freedom — no propagating modes, no energy landscape, no local observables. The framework's five primitives are all trivial or undefined for this starting point: the Tick has nothing local to propagate, the Field has no gradient to descend, the Attractor has no potential landscape, Resolution has nothing to resolve, and Conservation conserves nothing local. Yet this topological theory is not physically vacuous: it has a well-defined covariant phase space, boundary charges and their algebra, and can couple to point particles. The paper performs a complete Hamiltonian analysis, implements reality conditions, studies the G → 0 limit as a smooth scaling, and argues the formulation is well-suited for discretization and quantization. The framework strain is severe and multi-dimensional: (a) gravity — the framework's paradigmatic example of gradient dynamics ("mass rolling down curvature gradients") — is derived from a theory with no dynamics at all; (b) the generating operation (gauging a global symmetry to local) is a meta-theoretical operation that transforms the rules of computation, not a computation within fixed rules — the framework has no meta-computational primitive for this; (c) the G → 0 limit is continuous, meaning there is no sharp boundary between "the framework applies" and "the framework is undefined" — the framework's descriptions fade to vacuity without any discontinuity marking the transition; (d) the frame field (the geometric/dynamical data) exists already at the topological level but carries no local dynamics until gauging, meaning the substrate for dynamics is present before dynamics exists. The paper's explicit suitability for discretization and quantization makes this especially pointed: the topological-to-gravitational path is not a classical curiosity but a viable route to quantum gravity that begins where the framework has nothing to say.

**Paper 3: Transition from Statistical to Hardware-Limited Scaling in Photonic Quantum State Reconstruction** (arXiv: 2603.12235v1, selected for validation)

This paper reports the experimental discovery of a "Hardware Horizon" in photonic quantum state reconstruction: a sharp phase transition where reconstruction error shifts from the predicted statistical scaling O(M^{-1/2}) to a hardware-imposed floor determined by spectral distortions of the realized unitary ensemble. Through the Worldlines lens, this is consistent with the framework's claim that the computational substrate imposes limits on resolution fidelity — the physical hardware (substrate) sets a floor that no amount of statistical accumulation (computation within the substrate) can overcome. The phase transition from statistical to hardware-limited scaling could be interpreted as the transition from resolution-limited to substrate-limited computation. However, this interpretation provides zero differential predictive content. The result is entirely derivable from standard quantum information theory and photonic hardware characterization: the spectral distortion model, the decoupling of coherent distortion from dynamic decoherence, and the specific scaling law involving hardware parameters all follow from conventional physics without any reference to the Worldlines framework. The framework's vocabulary ("substrate limits resolution") merely redescribes what the paper already explains in precise, quantitative, falsifiable terms. The framework did not predict the specific form of the hardware horizon, the spectral distortion mechanism, the scaling exponents, the phase transition sharpness, or the distinction between coherent and decoherent error sources. This is post-hoc consistency of the weakest kind: the framework's qualitative claim ("the substrate limits computation") is confirmed, but this claim is so generic that any hardware limitation would confirm it. No genuine predictive validation occurs.

### FRAMEWORK STRAINS

The most severe strain this cycle comes from Paper 2's demonstration that gravity emerges from a topological theory via gauging. The strain is not merely that the framework cannot describe the topological starting point (already flagged as T058) or that it lacks meta-computational primitives for gauging (T057). The strain is deeper and more structural: **the framework's explanatory direction is backwards for its own paradigmatic example.**

The framework's showcase claim is that gravity is "mass rolling down the curvature gradient" — a dynamical process within a computational substrate. Paper 2 shows that this dynamical process is *generated* by a structural operation (gauging) applied to a theory with no local dynamics. The curvature gradient does not exist until gauging creates it. The rolling does not happen until gauging creates something to roll down. The substrate's dynamical character is not a given — it is produced by a specific algebraic operation on a topological starting point.

This means the framework's foundational ontological claim — that reality is computation, and structure (including spacetime geometry) is emergent from computation — is precisely inverted for gravity. Structure (topological BF theory + symmetry) is the starting point; dynamics (propagating gravitons, curvature gradients, the causal structure that gives the Tick its meaning) is what emerges. The framework cannot absorb this by saying "gauging is a form of computation" without trivializing "computation" to mean any mathematical operation, which would drain the core claim of all content.

Combined with Paper 1's demonstration that conservation laws are downstream of homotopy algebraic morphisms, this cycle establishes a pattern: the framework's two most fundamental primitives (Conservation of Computation and the Field/gradient dynamics exemplified by gravity) are both *derived* from algebraic/structural content that the framework treats as secondary or ignores entirely. The framework is not merely incomplete — its ontological hierarchy (computation first, structure emergent) may be inverted.

### NEW TENSIONS

**T059 — The ontological inversion: dynamics is derived from structure, not structure from dynamics**
**Opened:** this cycle
**Description:** Papers 1 and 2 jointly demonstrate that the framework's two most fundamental primitives — Conservation of Computation and the Field/gradient dynamics exemplified by gravity — are both derivable from algebraic and structural starting points that the framework treats as secondary or cannot express at all. Paper 2 shows gravity (the paradigmatic gradient dynamics) emerges from a topological theory via gauging. Paper 1 shows conservation laws (the paradigmatic conserved quantity) emerge from homotopy algebraic quasi-isomorphisms. The framework's ontological hierarchy is: computation (dynamics) is fundamental → structure (geometry, algebra) is emergent. The papers demonstrate the reverse: structure is fundamental → dynamics (including the specific dynamics the framework takes as primitive) is emergent. This is not a gap to be filled or a missing primitive to be added — it is evidence that the framework's foundational ontological ordering may be inverted. The framework describes downstream consequences (gradient descent, conservation) while being unable to describe the upstream generative structures (topological field theories, L∞-algebras, gauging operations) from which those consequences derive. This subsumes and sharpens T054 (dynamics from structure), T055 (conservation from morphisms), T057 (meta-computational primitives), and T058 (framework undefined for topological theories) into a single, more fundamental claim: the framework's explanatory direction is backwards.
**Why unresolved:** Resolving this would require either (a) demonstrating that the structural starting points (topological BF theories, L∞-algebras) themselves emerge from computation in a way that restores the framework's ontological ordering — a claim for which no evidence exists and which would need to explain what "computation" means in a regime with no local dynamics; (b) inverting the framework's ontology so that structure is primitive and dynamics/computation is derived, which would be a complete rewrite requiring new foundational primitives (topological invariants? algebraic categories? symmetry operations?); or (c) adopting a neutral position where structure and dynamics are co-fundamental, which would dissolve the framework's distinctive claim ("reality is literally computation") into a more modest and less interesting claim ("reality has both structural and dynamical aspects"). The G → 0 limit of Paper 2 makes this especially sharp: there is a continuous path from full gravitational dynamics to pure topological structure, and the framework's descriptive power degrades continuously along this path without any discontinuity that would mark a natural boundary of applicability.

**T060 — Dimensional transmutation of conservation: lower-dimensional conservation is generated by higher-dimensional structure**
**Opened:** this cycle
**Description:** Paper 1 derives the conservation structure (Lax connection, infinite conserved charges) of the 2D principal chiral model from a quasi-isomorphism with 4D semi-holomorphic Chern-Simons theory. The conservation laws of the lower-dimensional system are not intrinsic to it — they are the image of a morphism from a higher-dimensional theory. The framework's Conservation of Computation is dimension-independent: "computation cannot be created or destroyed" applies equally at all scales and in all dimensions. But this paper shows that the *existence* of conservation in a 2D system depends on its relationship to a 4D structure. If the 4D theory did not exist, or if the quasi-isomorphism did not exist, the 2D system would not be integrable and would not have an infinite tower of conserved charges. Conservation in the lower-dimensional theory is contingent on higher-dimensional algebraic structure, not on a universal primitive. This is distinct from T055 (which concerns the algebraic nature of the generating morphism) because T060 concerns the dimensional aspect: conservation laws can be "inherited" across dimensions via algebraic morphisms, meaning the framework's dimension-independent formulation of conservation misses the generative mechanism.
**Why unresolved:** The framework would need to either (a) explain how a dimension-independent conservation principle accounts for dimension-dependent conservation generation, (b) accept that conservation in specific theories is not a consequence of a universal primitive but of the specific algebraic relationships between theories in different dimensions, or (c) incorporate a theory of inter-dimensional algebraic relationships into the framework, for which no path through the existing primitives is visible. The broader implication is that "why is this system integrable?" is answered by "because it has the right algebraic relationship to a higher-dimensional theory," which is a structural answer, not a dynamical one.

### CYCLE ASSESSMENT

This cycle was highly generative but in a convergent rather than divergent direction. Papers 1 and 2 are not independent strains — they are two manifestations of the same fundamental challenge: the framework's ontological ordering (computation/dynamics first, structure emergent) is inverted relative to the actual derivational structure of physics. Paper 1 shows conservation emerging from algebra; Paper 2 shows dynamics emerging from topology. Together they compress the proliferating structural-content-gap tensions (T026, T054, T055, T057, T058) into a single sharp claim (T059: the framework's explanatory direction is backwards). This is valuable because it transforms a scattered constellation of related tensions into a focused challenge with clear resolution conditions. The validation paper (Paper 3) contributed nothing beyond the weakest form of post-hoc consistency, confirming the pattern that the framework generates no differential predictions about experimental results. The next cycle would be most productive if it engaged with papers that either (a) challenge T059 by demonstrating dynamics generating structure (rather than the reverse), which would create genuine dialectical tension rather than piling onto the structural-content critique, or (b) provide results where the framework's dynamical primitives succeed in ways that purely structural descriptions cannot match, restoring some balance to the ontological question. The system is currently in danger of circling the structural-content-gap basin rather than finding new gradient; papers from condensed matter, quantum information, or astrophysics — domains where dynamical descriptions have proven power — might provide more productive friction than further examples from mathematical physics where the structural perspective is historically dominant.
