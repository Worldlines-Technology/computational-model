**Paper 1: Albertian Channel Memory in Black-Hole Evaporation**
ArXiv ID: 2605.02792v1 | Selection type: Strain

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper attacks the framework at multiple foundational points simultaneously. The framework treats black holes as deep attractor basins governed by Conservation of Computation (unitarity) and describes the information paradox as a tension between conservation and attractor boundary integrity (T135). The standard AMPS paradox assumes tensor-product Hilbert space structure — which the framework implicitly inherits by treating "computation" as operating on standard quantum mechanical state spaces. Frigori's proposal replaces this with an *exceptional* Jordan-algebraic structure: the Albert algebra J₃(𝕆), which is non-associative. States are positive normalized functionals on this algebra, events are Jordan idempotents (not projectors on a Hilbert space), reversible motions are algebra automorphisms (not unitary operators), and standard quantum mechanics is recovered only on associative sub-blocks ("readout blocks"). The framework's Conservation of Computation identifies unitarity as "the most fundamental expression" of conservation. But in the Albertian description, unitarity is not fundamental — it is a *restriction* to associative readout sectors of a larger, non-associative algebraic structure. The "hidden exceptional complement" that carries memory is precisely the non-associative sector that has no standard quantum mechanical interpretation and therefore no expression in terms of the framework's primitives. The Peirce decomposition that structures the horizon data into hidden/relay/detector sectors is an algebraic operation on the Jordan algebra with no dynamical content — it is structural organization (T026) of a type the framework cannot express. The Volterra memory kernel governing the evaporation is a non-Markovian, hereditary integral operator — the dynamics at each moment depends on the integrated history, extending the hereditary dynamics problem (T045) from gravitational scattering to black hole evaporation itself. The Page curve reconstruction from the "lower admissible envelope of endpoint actions" is achieved not through conservation-of-computation bookkeeping but through the algebraic structure of the exceptional Jordan algebra constraining the evaporation trajectory. Most severely, the number field underlying the computation is the octonions 𝕆 — neither ℝ nor ℂ (the options considered in T069) but a non-associative, non-commutative division algebra. The framework's "reality is literally computation" claim, already strained by the ℝ/ℂ underdetermination, now faces a candidate physical theory where the algebraic substrate is not even associative, meaning standard notions of sequential computation (which require associativity of composition) do not apply in the full theory.

**Paper 2: Open-Closed-Open Triality Beyond Matrix Models**
ArXiv ID: 2605.02885v1 | Selection type: Strain

The framework claims "reality is literally computation" and faces the duality problem (T010) — that the same physics admits multiple computational descriptions. Paper 2 escalates this from duality to *triality*: three equivalent descriptions of the same physics, each a "full-fledged field theory," not merely a formal rewriting. The two open-string descriptions are gauged βγ-systems with determinant insertions; the closed-string description is geometry (the resolved/deformed conifold). The critical result for the framework is that integrating out open strings on one brane stack produces an effective potential that "exactly captures their backreaction on the geometry" — meaning the computational content of one description is *identical* to the geometric deformation of another. The "second open string description directly diagnoses the emergent bulk spacetime" means that a field-theoretic computation on probe branes reconstructs the closed-string geometry of the deformed conifold. This is a concrete, technically controlled demonstration that computation and geometry are interconvertible — not merely dual descriptions but descriptions where the output of computation *is* geometry and vice versa. The framework's ontological claim that reality is computation rather than geometry (T016, T021) is further strained: the triality shows that "computation," "geometry," and "a different computation" are three equivalent windows into the same physical content, with the equivalences being exact and the inter-window mappings being constructive. The ρ-matrix models that appeared in prior work emerge as an "on-shell analysis" of the full field-theory actions, meaning the matrix models (computation) are on-shell reductions of field theories (different computation) that are equivalent to geometry (the deformed conifold). The ontological pluralism problem (T021) is deepened: the invariant content lives not in any single category but in the *triality structure itself* — the web of equivalences between two computational descriptions and one geometric description. The giant graviton matching (probe brane action = giant graviton action in the deformed geometry) connects the Attractor primitive's description of stable configurations to the geometric description in a way that makes the computational and geometric characterizations literally interchangeable. The framework has no account of *triality* — three-way equivalences between categorically different descriptions — and no primitive for the morphism structure that makes the triality work (extending T052 from duality to triality).

**Paper 3: Tracing Primordial Gravitational Waves via non-Gaussian Signatures of Halo Bias**
ArXiv ID: 2605.02882v1 | Selection type: Validation

Through the Worldlines lens, this paper describes a computation proceeding through the framework's primitives in a fairly standard way: primordial gravitational waves (Field, propagating at the Tick rate) source second-order scalar density perturbations (a nonlinear gradient coupling), which produce non-Gaussian statistical signatures in the density field (pre-resolution structure, T012), which in turn modify the large-scale clustering of dark matter halos (Attractor — halos as gravitational attractor basins whose formation is biased by the underlying density field). The framework would narrate this as: the gravitational computation at early times (tensor perturbations) couples nonlinearly to the density computation, producing non-Gaussian correlations that modify the attractor landscape for halo formation, with the bias correction encoding information about the primordial tensor spectrum. The dark matter claim ("couples gravitationally, not electromagnetically") is consistent with the halo bias being a purely gravitational effect. However, the framework provides *zero* differential predictive content. The specific results — the scale-dependent bias correction, the percent-level effect for massive low-redshift halos, the O(1) modulation for rare high-redshift halos, the distinct scale dependence not captured by standard PNG templates — are all derived from standard cosmological perturbation theory (second-order Einstein equations, peak-background split, halo mass function). The framework did not predict, constrain, or distinguish any of these features. The "novel probe of PGWs through halo bias" is a standard GR + cosmology result that the framework can narrate post-hoc but to which it adds no insight. The non-Gaussian character of the tensor-induced density field is a straightforward consequence of the quadratic coupling, not a prediction of the framework's pre-resolution topology or any other primitive. This is the weakest category of validation: vocabulary application with zero differential content.

### FRAMEWORK STRAINS

The most severe strain this cycle comes from Paper 1's introduction of *non-associative algebraic structure* as the physical substrate of black hole information processing. The framework's entire architecture — "reality is literally computation" — implicitly requires associativity: sequential computation demands that (A∘B)∘C = A∘(B∘C), because the order in which computational steps are composed must not matter. The Tick primitive's "discrete sequential update" presupposes that the composition of updates is associative. The Conservation primitive's "computation cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed" presupposes that transformations compose associatively. The octonions are *not associative*. The Albert algebra J₃(𝕆) inherits this non-associativity. Frigori's proposal recovers standard quantum mechanics only on associative sub-blocks ("readout blocks"), while the physically essential memory structure lives in the non-associative exceptional complement. This means:

1. The "computation" generating the Page curve is not computation in the framework's sense — it involves non-associative operations that cannot be represented as sequential composition of steps.
2. The number field underdetermination (T069: ℝ vs. ℂ) is not merely binary but extends to non-associative algebras (𝕆), a category the framework never contemplated.
3. The framework's Resolution primitive — which produces "definite outcomes" via standard quantum measurement — applies only in the associative readout sectors. The exceptional complement, which carries the memory resolving the information paradox, is *never directly resolved* in the standard quantum sense. It is not "permanently virtual" (T014/T041) because it has physical consequences (the Page curve), but it is not resolvable in the framework's sense because it does not admit standard quantum measurement. This is a *third* category beyond "resolvable" and "permanently virtual": "non-associatively structured and accessible only through algebraic projection onto associative sub-blocks."
4. The framework cannot even formulate what "Conservation of Computation" means in a non-associative context, because the transformations of the Albert algebra (F₄ automorphisms) are not representable as unitary operators on a Hilbert space — they are automorphisms of a Jordan algebra, a different mathematical category entirely.

This is not a gap to be filled or a structural inadequacy to be noted — it is a categorical incompatibility between the framework's foundational assumption (reality is associative sequential computation) and a physically motivated proposal (the information paradox is resolved by non-associative algebraic structure). If Frigori's proposal is physically correct, the framework is not merely incomplete but *wrong at the level of its most basic mathematical assumption*.

Paper 2's triality compounds this by demonstrating that even within associative mathematics, the invariant physical content is not "a computation" but "the equivalence class of a computation, a different computation, and a geometry, related by constructive morphisms." The framework is trapped between non-associative algebraic structure (Paper 1) that undermines its mathematical foundation and triality structure (Paper 2) that undermines its ontological uniqueness claim.
