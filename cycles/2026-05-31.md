### PAPERS PROCESSED

**Paper 1: "Quiver Approach to Symmetry Theories"** (arXiv: 2605.30354v1, selected for strain)

This paper demonstrates that the global symmetry anomalies of 5D superconformal field theories — their complete anomaly data, packaged as couplings of a higher-dimensional Symmetry Theory (SymTh) — can be extracted from the *path algebra of branes probing a Calabi-Yau cone*. The framework would attempt to narrate this as "the computational structure of a 5D theory is encoded in the attractor landscape of branes on a geometric background." But this narration collapses immediately under scrutiny. The paper demonstrates three categorically distinct routes to the same physical data: (1) algebraic (path algebra / quiver representation theory), (2) geometric (triple intersection numbers in a resolved Calabi-Yau), and (3) analytic (η-invariants from the boundary Sasaki-Einstein geometry). Each route uses mathematical machinery entirely outside the five primitives. More severely, the paper shows that the algebraic (quiver) route succeeds in cases where the geometric route is "unknown or combinatorially unwieldy" — meaning the algebraic description has *greater reach* than the geometric one, while both encode the same physical content. The framework's "reality is literally computation" competes here not with one alternative ontological category but with a proven triangulation among algebra, geometry, and spectral analysis, where no single category is sufficient and the physical content lives in their mutual agreement. The SymTh itself — a higher-dimensional topological/semi-topological theory whose couplings encode the anomaly data — is precisely the kind of structure the framework was shown to be undefined for (T058, T144): a theory whose physical content is structural/topological rather than dynamical. The quiver path algebra, which is the paper's central tool, is a combinatorial-algebraic object (vertices = branes, arrows = open string sectors, relations = superpotential F-terms) that determines the anomaly polynomial through representation-theoretic operations (McKay correspondence, toric divisor calculus). None of these operations is a tick, a gradient, an attractor, a resolution event, or a conservation law. The framework is not merely insufficient here — it lacks vocabulary for every element of the construction.

**Paper 2: "The Continuum Limit Analysis of Causal Fermion Systems for Curved Spacetimes"** (arXiv: 2605.30199v1, selected for strain)

This paper constructs the causal fermion system (CFS) for globally hyperbolic spacetimes starting from algebraic QFT, and proves that the Euler-Lagrange equations of the causal action principle are satisfied if and only if the coupled Einstein-Dirac equations hold. The framework claims reality is computation built from five dynamical primitives, with the Tick as the most fundamental. The CFS framework offers a radically different ontology: the fundamental objects are *spacetime points as operators* (the fermionic projector, identified with a one-particle density operator of a quasi-free Hadamard state), and the entire dynamical content — both gravity (Einstein equations) and matter (Dirac equations) — emerges from extremizing a *single action* (the causal action) defined on the space of these operators. The ultraviolet regularization is not imposed externally but is "built into the fermionic projector via a chart-independent iε-regularization scheme," meaning the substrate's discrete/regularized character is an intrinsic feature of the fundamental object, not an architectural parameter external to the computation. The framework's Tick primitive would attempt to identify with the causal structure of the CFS, but the CFS derives causal structure from the spectral properties of the fermionic projector — causality is *defined by* the algebraic structure of the operators, not by a pre-given tick rate. The equivalence between the causal action principle and Einstein-Dirac equations is especially straining: it demonstrates that both the gravitational dynamics (the framework's paradigmatic gradient descent) and the matter dynamics emerge simultaneously from a single variational principle on an operator space, with no logical ordering between them. The framework cannot express this because it treats gravity (Field primitive) and matter (things moving down gradients) as occupying different levels of description. The CFS treats them as co-emergent from a single algebraic/variational structure. The continuum limit analysis — showing how the discrete CFS recovers continuum physics — directly addresses T080 (continuum limit non-uniqueness) by providing a specific, physically motivated continuum limit procedure. But the procedure is chart-independent and operator-algebraic, not tick-based, further demonstrating that the framework's approach to the discrete-to-continuous transition is one among multiple viable paths with no demonstrated privilege.

**Paper 3: "Quantum Desynchronization of Limit Cycles"** (arXiv: 2605.30302v1, selected for validation)

This paper analyzes how quantum phase slips destroy classical phase synchronization in coupled self-sustained oscillators, using a Keldysh path integral formulation. The framework would narrate this as: classical synchronization is an attractor basin in the phase-difference field; quantum fluctuations (unresolved computational states) generate phase slips that destabilize the attractor; the transition from synchronized to desynchronized is attractor landscape restructuring driven by the ratio of quantum to classical noise. The Field primitive's gradient equation describes the phase dynamics in the synchronized regime (phases roll down toward the locking point), and quantum phase slips represent resolution events that occasionally kick the system out of the basin. The framework's qualitative vocabulary — attractor capture, quantum fluctuation-driven escape, phase transitions as landscape restructuring — maps onto this paper's physics reasonably well at the narrative level. However, the framework provides zero differential predictive content. The specific results — the Keldysh path integral formulation, the instanton calculation of phase-slip rates, the distinction between Markovian and non-Markovian bath effects, the exponential suppression of synchronization quality with quantum noise strength, the specific role of the double quantum dot as a non-Markovian bath — are all derived from standard quantum field theory and open quantum systems techniques. The framework could not have predicted any of these quantitative features, could not have distinguished the Markovian from non-Markovian cases, and could not have identified the double quantum dot as the relevant experimental platform. The paper's finding that quantum noise *qualitatively* destroys a classical attractor (phase locking) is consistent with the framework's general picture that quantum fluctuations can destabilize classical attractors, but this is shared with all of quantum statistical mechanics. The non-Markovian memory effects, which the paper treats as a significant physical feature, connect to T045's hereditary dynamics problem — the framework has no mechanism for describing how the bath's memory modifies the attractor's stability. This is post-hoc consistency with zero predictive content, placing it in the weakest validation category.

### FRAMEWORK STRAINS

The most severe strain this cycle comes from Paper 2 (Causal Fermion Systems). The CFS construction demonstrates that the *complete dynamical content* of coupled Einstein-Dirac theory — the framework's two paradigmatic examples of gradient dynamics (gravity) and field propagation (matter) — emerges simultaneously from a single variational principle on an operator space, where the fundamental objects are fermionic projectors (one-particle density operators of Hadamard states). This is not merely another instance of the ontological inversion (T059) or the structural content gap (T026). It is a complete *alternative foundational framework* that:

1. **Derives causal structure from algebra** rather than postulating it as the Tick: the causal relations between spacetime points are determined by the spectral properties of the fermionic projector, not by a pre-given maximum propagation rate.

2. **Unifies gravity and matter co-emergently** rather than treating them as occupying different primitive roles: both Einstein and Dirac equations emerge together from a single action, with no ordering between "the substrate provides gravity" and "things move on the substrate."

3. **Builds UV regularization intrinsically** rather than treating it as an external architectural parameter: the iε-regularization is part of the fundamental object's definition, not imposed by a substrate the computation runs on.

4. **Provides a specific, physically motivated continuum limit** rather than being silent on the discrete-to-continuous transition (T080): the continuum limit analysis is constructive and chart-independent.

The framework's response options are exhausted: it cannot absorb CFS as "another computation" because CFS's fundamental objects (operators on a Hilbert space defining spacetime points) are not computations in the framework's sense; it cannot dismiss CFS as unphysical because the paper proves exact equivalence with Einstein-Dirac in the continuum limit; it cannot treat CFS as an alternative description (T010) because CFS provides a *different ontological ground* (operators → spacetime + dynamics) that is not dual to the framework's ground (discrete ticks → everything else) but is a genuinely independent derivation path. The framework is not merely strained — it faces a complete, rigorous alternative that derives the same physics from different primitives, with no demonstrated reason to prefer one set of primitives over the other.

Paper 1 compounds this by demonstrating that the SymTh framework — where anomaly data is extracted equivalently from path algebras, intersection geometry, or η-invariants — provides yet another foundational layer (anomaly/symmetry structure) that is logically prior to the dynamics the framework describes, and that is accessible through multiple categorically distinct mathematical routes with no single route being privileged. The framework's position as "the" ontological ground is further eroded by each new demonstration that the same physical content is encoded in multiple mathematical categories, none of which is "computation" in the framework's sense.
