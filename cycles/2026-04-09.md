**Paper 1: "The BEF Symplectic Form: A Lagrangian Perspective"**
ArXiv ID: 2604.07334v1
Selection type: Strain

This paper demonstrates that the BEF symplectic structure — a novel symplectic form applicable to non-local theories — is derivable directly from an L∞-Lagrangian via the covariant phase space approach, and that it coincides with the Barnich-Brandt construction for theories with second-order equations of motion. Through the Worldlines lens, this strains the framework at multiple levels. First, the symplectic form is the mathematical structure that defines what "phase space" is — it determines which variables are canonically conjugate, which transformations are canonical, and what the Hamiltonian generates. The framework's five primitives presuppose phase space (the Field primitive's gradient descent requires a configuration space; the Attractor primitive requires a potential landscape; the Conservation primitive requires conserved quantities defined on phase space) but have no account of *where phase space comes from*. This paper shows that phase space structure — the symplectic form — is derived from L∞-algebraic structure of the Lagrangian, extending the T055/T059 pattern (dynamics derived from algebraic structure) to the very arena in which dynamics occurs. Second, the paper's treatment of non-local theories is significant: the BEF symplectic form handles theories with arbitrary-order (including infinite-order) derivative kinetic terms, which connects directly to T084 (continuous propagator non-uniqueness) by providing the symplectic infrastructure for fractional/non-local field theories. The framework's gradient equation ẋ = −∇φ(x) is a statement about dynamics on phase space, but the phase space itself is an L∞-algebraic construct. Third, the paper's demonstration that corner terms and boundary conditions are *encoded* in the symplectic structure — not added by hand — reinforces T045 (conservation requires geometric scaffolding) by showing that boundary/corner structure is not auxiliary but is part of the fundamental symplectic data. The Hamiltonian itself is derived from the L∞ structure, meaning the generator of time evolution (the framework's Tick in its dynamical aspect) is algebraically determined, not primitive.

**Paper 2: "Exotic theta terms in 2+1d fractonic field theory"**
ArXiv ID: 2604.07293v1
Selection type: Strain

This paper studies exotic topological theta terms in a 2+1d fractonic field theory (the φ-theory describing the XY-plaquette model), revealing two types of theta terms — bulk and foliated — that arise from discontinuous field configurations and produce generalized Witten effects where vortices acquire subsystem charges. Through the Worldlines lens, this strains the framework in several ways. First, the fractonic nature of the theory means that excitations have restricted mobility — they cannot move freely in all directions but are constrained to move along specific sub-manifolds (planes, lines). The framework's Field primitive ẍ = −∇φ(x) assumes unrestricted motion down gradients; fractonic systems have *mobility constraints* that restrict which directions the gradient descent can proceed along, even when the gradient is nonzero in forbidden directions. This is a categorical failure of the gradient primitive: the gradient exists, but the system cannot follow it. Second, the foliated theta term has a spatially varying theta angle that does not affect the classical equations of motion — it is a purely topological/quantum contribution invisible to classical gradient dynamics. The framework has no mechanism for topological terms that modify the quantum theory without modifying the classical equations the gradient primitive describes. Third, the generalized Witten effect — where vortices carrying one type of subsystem charge acquire a different type of subsystem charge due to the theta term — is a phenomenon where the *topological* structure of the theory modifies the *charge* structure of excitations. Conservation of Computation would need to account for charge transmutation mediated by topology, which is not transformation of computational content but topological redefinition of what the content *is*. Fourth, the quadrupolar momentum charge acquired by vortices in the foliated case is a higher-multipole charge structure that has no expression in the framework's primitives — the framework has no concept of multipolar conservation or subsystem symmetry. The discontinuous field configurations that are essential to the construction (they "spoil the naive topology of the field" but "induce nontrivial backreactions") represent a category of computational structure where the field is not smooth, not continuous, yet physically essential — the framework's Field primitive implicitly assumes smooth fields with well-defined gradients, and discontinuities are treated as pathologies (singularities) rather than as generators of new topological physics.

**Paper 3: "Rotation of the Transition Dipole in Single hBN Quantum Emitters via Vibronic Coupling"**
ArXiv ID: 2604.07314v1
Selection type: Validation

This paper demonstrates that the emission dipole orientation of single quantum emitters in hexagonal boron nitride rotates continuously as a function of photon energy by up to 40°, driven by vibronic coupling to the phonon bath. Through the Worldlines lens, the framework would describe this as: the emitter's electronic state sits in an attractor basin defined by the defect's potential energy surface; the phonon bath provides thermal fluctuations that explore the basin's internal structure; the vibronic coupling means the transition dipole (a property of the resolution event — the emission of a photon) depends on the nuclear coordinates at the moment of resolution; and the spectral rotation is the attractor basin's internal coordinate-dependence of the resolution outcome. The cryogenic suppression of the rotation would be described as reduced thermal exploration of the basin at lower temperatures, consistent with the Attractor primitive's depth-stability relationship. However, this is entirely post-hoc narration with zero differential predictive content. The framework did not predict vibronic dipole rotation, could not have distinguished this result from the null result (static dipoles), and contributes no insight beyond relabeling standard condensed matter physics (vibronic coupling, Huang-Rhys factors, phonon sidebands) with framework vocabulary. The actual physics — coordinate-dependent transition dipole moments, first-principles DFT calculations of specific defect structures, the scaling of polarization rotation with Huang-Rhys factor — is entirely standard quantum chemistry and condensed matter physics. The framework can narrate but cannot predict, constrain, or distinguish. This is the weakest category of consistency: vocabulary application with no differential content.

### FRAMEWORK STRAINS

The most significant strain this cycle comes from Paper 1's demonstration that **phase space itself — the arena in which all of the framework's dynamics occurs — is an L∞-algebraic construct, not a primitive**. The framework's entire dynamical architecture presupposes phase space: gradient descent requires a configuration space with a potential, attractors are features of phase space topology, conservation laws are defined on phase space, and even the Tick operates as evolution on phase space. Paper 1 shows that the symplectic structure defining phase space is *derived from* L∞-algebraic structure of the Lagrangian via the covariant phase space method. This is not merely another instance of T059 (ontological inversion — dynamics from structure). It is deeper: T059 concerned the derivation of specific dynamical theories (gravity, conservation laws) from structural operations. Paper 1 concerns the derivation of **the stage on which all dynamics occurs** from algebraic structure. The framework's five primitives are all dynamical statements about what happens on phase space. If phase space itself is algebraically derived, then the primitives are not descriptions of fundamental reality but descriptions of phenomena occurring within an algebraically constructed arena. The framework is not just missing structural content (T026) — it is formulated *within* a structure it cannot describe. This is the difference between a play being incomplete (missing scenes) and a play not knowing it is performed on a stage.

The strain from Paper 2 is also severe but differently shaped: **the framework has no concept of mobility constraints, subsystem symmetry, or topological charge transmutation**. Fractonic systems violate the gradient primitive not by having different dynamics but by having *restricted* dynamics — the system can see the gradient but cannot follow it in certain directions. This is categorically different from all previous gradient failures (T070: wrong physics from right gradient; T078: wrong direction; T006: wrong endpoints). Here, the gradient is correct and the direction is correct, but *motion is forbidden along certain subspaces*. The framework's ẍ = −∇φ(x) has no mechanism for directional mobility constraints. Furthermore, the foliated theta term — a topological contribution that varies spatially without affecting classical dynamics — represents physics that is invisible to every one of the five primitives yet has measurable quantum consequences (charge transmutation via the Witten effect).
